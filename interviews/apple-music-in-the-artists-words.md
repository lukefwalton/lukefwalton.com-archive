Apple Music prints this under **In the Artist's Words** on the [Scoobert Doobert artist page](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/scoobert-doobert/1240946356) (store id 1240946356). The profile does not date the Q&A. This copy was taken from the US storefront on **September 25, 2026**. The year on this page is the capture date.

The prompts are Apple's. The answers are his.

### Describe one of your earliest musical memories in detail.

I was Older Simba in the Lion King for our elementary school performance. My voice cracked.

### What are your top 3 all-time favorite albums on Apple Music, and why?

Alabama Shakes, *Sound & Color*

Kimbra, *The Golden Echo*

Mother Mother, *Eureka*

Without these records, I wouldn't make the music I do, mix the way I do, or even be the same person. To Pimp a Butterfly, Get to Heaven, and Harmony of Difference should be on here, too, among many, many others.

### Find a song of yours on Apple Music that you love and tell us what makes it special.

["Feels So Good"](/songs/feels-so-good/)

I wrote this song thinking about my best friends, chilling on Moonlight Beach, beer and bud in hand. I love writing Platonic love songs. They're real celebrations of why I love life. The homies!

### What's one of the most memorable moments in your career so far?

I started learning Japanese when I was very sick in the hospital with Guillain-Barré syndrome. I didn't know why. It just brought me comfort. Now, I produce a band called [CHAI](/with/chai/) from Nagoya, and spend every chance I get in Japan. Serendipity and joy!

### Is there anything left on your bucket list as an artist or band?

I would love to collaborate with my musical heroes. I hope to keep getting my chops up, so I can hang when that time comes! I want to grow the community / scene and create with the people I love. I also wanna play Red Rocks!
