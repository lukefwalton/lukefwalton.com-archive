A note from Luke F. Walton, founder of Surmado.

We recently migrated and optimized a site for GV Roofing Solutions in Northern California. The owner said something I loved: "I'm a roofer, not a web developer."

Exactly. Surmado Sites isn't another Wix or Squarespace, where you, the owner, have to manage the site. Surmado takes care of it. We handle all the security updates, and all the alphabet soup of things you shouldn't have to know like SEO, GEO, and DNS. That way you can focus on your business, while having a website that represents it well.

But we didn't want to be the standard web development company or agency. With Surmado's AI agent, you don't have to learn an editor or wait days for a simple change. Don't like anything? Just tell it what you want and it's fixed in about a minute. You approve everything before it goes live. You're not fighting with templates. You're just saying what you want to change, and Surmado knows your site and your business, and learns every time you use it.

We don't stop there either. We can translate your site into up to 15 languages, host multiple sites under one roof (pun intended), and even automate blog posts so your business has more useful content for search and AI.

Surmado actually started when a family friend's business wasn't discoverable on ChatGPT. Despite being some of the best Mexican food in San Diego (and celiac-safe), he didn't show up. We fixed that, and now tourists choose his restaurant thanks to ChatGPT's recommendations. See what that looked like. A Surmado Site helps your business be more legible to AI, so you can get that business.

Just like a great team, everyone has a position to play. You don't expect your outfielder to be a great pitcher! (Unless you're Ohtani!) Surmado is here to fill in the gap. We keep things simple, keep them off your plate, and make your website work for you. We're a dedicated team of six folks in Phoenix, Arizona and Southern California who love small business deeply, and want you to win.

We'd be honored to rebuild your site for free and show you what our team can do for you. Only pay if you like it.

If you ever have a question, my email is always open: luke@surmado.com

— Luke F. Walton, Founder, Surmado
