Refried Being is a seven-track album by Burrito Bot, released April 26, 2024, on Beformer, about nine minutes. Burrito Bot is a separate DistroKid artist from Scoobert Doobert. Wrap Music followed on June 14. Spotify lists Burrito Dream, Burrito in the Day (Jacuzzi in the Night), Hangry, Chipotle Secret (Double Meat Surprise), Burrito Time, Hangry (Reprise), and Burrito Baby.

The project essay is I Made 1000 AI Music Songs So You Don't Have to (July 2, 2024), the same day as the Love Music More episode. It names Wrap Music as the second LP.
