**Wrap Music** is a 15-track album by Burrito Bot, released June 14, 2024, on Beformer, about 23 minutes. Burrito Bot is a separate DistroKid artist from Scoobert Doobert, an AI burrito project. The cover was generated with Runway. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/49QQ8dgQL5VS476ThckL66) spells the titles as released, including Tomatillo (Es Mi Perferida) and Guacamole Recipe (Receipe).

[I Made 1000 AI Music Songs So You Don't Have to](https://lovemusicmore.substack.com/p/i-made-1000-ai-music-songs-so-you) (July 2, 2024) is the project essay. It calls this the second LP, and it is the same day as the [Love Music More episode](/love-music-more/episodes/bbl-drizzy-and-burrito-bot-ai-song-generation-tips/). [*Refried Being*](/albums/refried-being/) is the first.
