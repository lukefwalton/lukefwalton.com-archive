**Plague Beats, Vol. 2**, six-track beat tape, **May 14, 2021**, ~one minute per track. Luke Francis Walton solo; sequel to [**Plague Beats, Vol. 1**](/albums/plague-beats-vol-1/) (September 2020). Same pandemic-era internet-native logic as [*Masks and Monsters*](/albums/masks-and-monsters/): meme titles as songs, minimal runtime, maximum title-as-content. On **October 5, 2026**, Spotify showed *Better Every Loop* 2,388 (1:01), *Instant Regret* 5,393 (0:47), *Looking Kinda Sus (Among Us)* 15,674 (1:00), and *Shitty Robots* 1,988 (1:00). *Cringe* and *Woah Dude* had no play count shown. The counts move.

Five lyric tracks have annotation pages. **Closer:** *Woah Dude*: **instrumental** (no song-meaning page; nothing to quote). Title riffs stoner / Keanu *whoa* meme energy as beat-tape outro.
