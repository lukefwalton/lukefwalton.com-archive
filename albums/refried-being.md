**Refried Being** is a seven-track album by Burrito Bot, released April 26, 2024, on Beformer, about nine minutes. Burrito Bot is a separate DistroKid artist from Scoobert Doobert. *Wrap Music* followed on June 14. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/5YhkIcucODXqa9qCT7JCAp) lists Burrito Dream, Burrito in the Day (Jacuzzi in the Night), Hangry, Chipotle Secret (Double Meat Surprise), Burrito Time, Hangry (Reprise), and Burrito Baby.

The project essay is [I Made 1000 AI Music Songs So You Don't Have to](https://lovemusicmore.substack.com/p/i-made-1000-ai-music-songs-so-you) (July 2, 2024), the same day as the [Love Music More episode](/love-music-more/episodes/bbl-drizzy-and-burrito-bot-ai-song-generation-tips/). It names *Wrap Music* as the second LP.
