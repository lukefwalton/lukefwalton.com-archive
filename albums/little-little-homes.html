Content

Little Little Homes is the 2016 FEiN EP that preceded the Little Homes album (May 31, 2016). Fresh Beats 365 reviewed it on March 10, 2016, so it was out by then. Spotify still lists it: 2016, four songs, © 2016 Unsigned, ℗ 2016 Unsigned.

On October 5, 2026, the Spotify page showed 274,251 plays on Sculptor (3:55), 74,712 on Girl You Can't Hide It (Album Version) (3:23), 31,022 on Outro (1:27), and 3,954,072 on #Grownupz (Album Version) (3:42) (screenshot). The #Grownupz figure matches FEiN Times (Issue #1). The Girl You Can't Hide It figure matches FEiN Times (Issue #2). Those matching numbers are the counts on the pages, not extra plays to add together.
