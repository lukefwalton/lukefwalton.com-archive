The Other Side: 2009 EP by Luke Walton feat. Blue Suburbia (~17:40 per archived lukewaltonband.com). High-school blues band lineup, not The Luke Walton Band (USC cohort, 2009–2011).

Blue Suburbia was a blues trio at La Costa Canyon, about 2008 to 2009. Luke Walton (guitar, vocals), Jordan Sorokin (bass), and Zach Freeman (drums) co-founded it. It followed Casey McSocial, his first band, and Mannequin, which he also co-founded. They won the school's Battle of the Bands. The solo EPs Just A Friend and Heart in Hand were going at the same time. The band's page is here.

The archived discography names that trio. A Wikipedia article on The Luke Walton Band listed the same roster. It was deleted July 16, 2026. The June 18, 2026 capture is what remains. Wikidata does not name the members.

The songs are Preschool, a Walton original, Move By Yourself (about 5:52, a Donavon Frankenreiter cover), Let It Rock (about 3:32, Kevin Rudolf featuring Lil Wayne), and I Don't Need No Doctor (about 4:46, a John Scofield and John Mayer arrangement of the Ray Charles song).

On August 20, 2009 they recorded at Soundboard Studio West in Rancho Bernardo. At the console: Jordan Sorokin, Zach Freeman, Luke Walton. Another frame is the three of them giving notes to the mix engineer. The engineer is not in the photo, and is not named.

A Preschool single was also listed on its own in 2009, at 3:40. The song later showed up on the June 2010 Charitable Chords compilation as Luke Walton (Blue Suburbia), and again on Goodbye/Hello in 2010.

Chronology: Just A Friend (2007) → Heart in Hand (2008) → The Other Side (2009) → Goodbye/Hello (2010).
