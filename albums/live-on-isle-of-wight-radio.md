**Live on Isle of Wight Radio**, three tracks by **Exist Elsewhere**. [Bandcamp](https://existelsewhere.bandcamp.com/album/live-on-isle-of-wight-radio) lists the release as **November 1, 2012**: [**The Great Beyond**](/songs/the-great-beyond/) (4:34), [**Digital Life**](/songs/digital-life/) (3:50), [**Wavelengths**](/songs/wavelengths/) (4:32). The session this site dates to **October 14, 2012**, recorded for Isle of Wight Radio at the **House of Rock**, 2009 La Mesa Drive, Santa Monica. **Aaron Dowler** on bass. He was the bassist before **Nick Petrou** and is not on [*413*](/albums/413/).

The masters are free here. The Pro Tools bounce is dated **October 18, 2012**. The file for the third track is named “Wavelenghts”; the Bandcamp title is **Wavelengths**.
