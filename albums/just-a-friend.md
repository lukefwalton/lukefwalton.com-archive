*Just A Friend* is a solo EP from Luke Walton's teenage years. Luke Francis Walton, not the basketball coach. He recorded it in 2006 and released it in 2007. The [archived discography](https://web.archive.org/web/20110714002356/http://lukewaltonband.com/site/CDs_and_Lyrics.html) times it at about 17 minutes and 40 seconds. [Amazon Music](https://www.amazon.com/Just-Friend-Luke-Walton/dp/B000XXWXF4) lists July 16, 2007, which is likely a later store date.

In 2008 he played it on KUSI News in San Diego. The photos are the desk, the hosts holding the CD. The hosts are not named. That appearance is a different day from the later [Dead End Love clip on KUSI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bSV8LbB8K-I).

He played guitar, piano, and sang, and he wrote the songs. Six tracks, with [lyrics on the old site](https://web.archive.org/web/20110714002356/http://lukewaltonband.com/site/CDs_and_Lyrics.html): [Prelude](/songs/prelude/) (instrumental), [One Take](/songs/one-take/), [Just A Friend](/songs/just-a-friend/), [Preschool](/songs/preschool/), [Who Needs Tears](/songs/who-needs-tears/), and [Dirty Blonde](/songs/dirty-blonde/).

The EP ran alongside the high-school bands: the Rock I IV V club in 2006, then [Casey McSocial](/with/casey-mcsocial/), Mannequin, and [Blue Suburbia](/with/blue-suburbia/). The solo records after this are [*Heart in Hand*](/albums/heart-in-hand/) (2008) and [*Goodbye/Hello*](/albums/goodbye-hello/) (2010, The Luke Walton Band). Later he recorded with [FEiN](/albums/little-homes-fein/) and as Scoobert Doobert.
