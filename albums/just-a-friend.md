**Early archive**, self-released solo EP, recorded in **2006** and released in **2007**, from **Luke Walton**’s (**Luke Francis Walton**; not the NBA coach) teenage songwriter period. Documented on [archived lukewaltonband.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20110714002356/http://lukewaltonband.com/site/CDs_and_Lyrics.html) (~17:40); [Amazon Music](https://www.amazon.com/Just-Friend-Luke-Walton/dp/B000XXWXF4) lists **July 16, 2007** (likely redistribution metadata). In **2008** he was on **KUSI News** in San Diego to perform in support of the EP. The photos are the desk, with the hosts holding the CD. The hosts are not named. That appearance is not the later [Dead End Love clip on KUSI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bSV8LbB8K-I). Walton later recorded with [The Luke Walton Band](/albums/goodbye-hello/) and [FEiN](/albums/little-homes-fein/), and as Scoobert Doobert.

**Luke Walton**, guitar, piano, voice, songwriting. Six tracks with [archived lyrics](https://web.archive.org/web/20110714002356/http://lukewaltonband.com/site/CDs_and_Lyrics.html): [**Prelude**](/songs/prelude/) (instrumental) · [**One Take**](/songs/one-take/) · [**Just A Friend**](/songs/just-a-friend/) · [**Preschool**](/songs/preschool/) · [**Who Needs Tears**](/songs/who-needs-tears/) · [**Dirty Blonde**](/songs/dirty-blonde/).

Chronology: parallel band line at La Costa Canyon (solo EPs ran at the same time): Rock I IV V club (2006) · <a href="/with/casey-mcsocial/">Casey McSocial</a> (first band) · **Mannequin** (co-founded) · <a href="/with/blue-suburbia/">Blue Suburbia</a> (co-founded) ([*The Other Side*](/albums/the-other-side/), 2009). Solo releases: [*Heart in Hand*](/albums/heart-in-hand/) (2008) → [*Goodbye/Hello*](/albums/goodbye-hello/) (2010, The Luke Walton Band).
