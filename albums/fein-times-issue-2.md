**FEiN Times (Issue #2)** is the 2015 FEiN release between [Issue #1](/albums/fein-times-issue-1/) and [*Little Little Homes*](/albums/little-little-homes/): [Girl You Can't Hide It](/songs/girl-you-cant-hide-it/), [Creatures](/songs/creatures/), and [Lonely People](/songs/lonely-people/). [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/1QRpK2GejShLkjZs9JJzei) files the three songs as a 2015 single, 11 minutes 28 seconds, © 2015 FEiN, ℗ 2015 FEiN. It is still up.

On **October 5, 2026**, that page showed **74,712** plays on Girl You Can't Hide It (3:26), **49,797** on Creatures (4:18), and **64,419** on Lonely People (3:44) ([screenshot](/evidence/fein-times-issue-2-spotify-2026-10-05.png)). [*Little Little Homes*](/albums/little-little-homes/) shows the same 74,712 figure on Girl You Can't Hide It (Album Version). That is one count on two pages, not a sum.
