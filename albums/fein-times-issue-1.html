Content

FEiN Times (Issue #1) is the first FEiN release: two songs, August 25, 2014, #Grownupz and Monay Grabba. Spotify files it as a 2014 EP, 7 minutes 3 seconds, © 2014 Unsigned, ℗ 2014 Unsigned. Apple Music files the same release as a single. It is still up.

On October 5, 2026, the Spotify page showed 3,954,072 plays on #Grownupz (3:44) and 33,116 on Monay Grabba (3:18) (screenshot). Little Little Homes shows the same 3,954,072 figure on #Grownupz (Album Version). That is one count on two pages, not a sum.

The newspaper cover is the Issue #1 zine. The songs later sit on Little Homes (May 31, 2016). FEiN Times (Issue #2) is the 2015 follow-up.
