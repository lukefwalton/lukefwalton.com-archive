Content

Live on Isle of Wight Radio, three tracks by Exist Elsewhere. Bandcamp lists the release as November 1, 2012: The Great Beyond (4:34), Digital Life (3:50), Wavelengths (4:32). The session this site dates to October 14, 2012, recorded for Isle of Wight Radio at the House of Rock, 2009 La Mesa Drive, Santa Monica. Aaron Dowler on bass. He was the bassist before Nick Petrou and is not on 413.

The masters are free here. The Pro Tools bounce is dated October 18, 2012. The file for the third track is named “Wavelenghts”; the Bandcamp title is Wavelengths.
