**FEiN Times (Issue #1)** is the first FEiN release: two songs, **August 25, 2014**, [#Grownupz](/songs/grownupz/) and [Monay Grabba](/songs/monay-grabba/). [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/49iMqu8K9R9TucQAzh8Gkq) files it as a 2014 EP, 7 minutes 3 seconds, © 2014 Unsigned, ℗ 2014 Unsigned. [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/fein-times-issue-1-single/907610072) files the same release as a single. It is still up.

On **October 5, 2026**, the Spotify page showed **3,954,072** plays on #Grownupz (3:44) and **33,116** on Monay Grabba (3:18) ([screenshot](/evidence/fein-times-issue-1-spotify-2026-10-05.png)). [*Little Little Homes*](/albums/little-little-homes/) shows the same 3,954,072 figure on #Grownupz (Album Version). That is one count on two pages, not a sum.

The newspaper cover is the Issue #1 zine. The songs later sit on [*Little Homes*](/albums/little-homes-fein/) (May 31, 2016). [FEiN Times (Issue #2)](/albums/fein-times-issue-2/) is the 2015 follow-up.
