Content

Heart in Hand is the 2008 solo EP, the follow-up to Just A Friend. Luke played guitar and piano, sang, and wrote the songs. The archived discography times it at about 13 minutes. The early solo releases are in the catalog.

The four songs are Heart in Hand, Out of (This) Place, Storyline, and Dead End Love. Several of them show up again on the 2010 Luke Walton Band LP Goodbye/Hello, some with new lyrics, and on YouTube from a December 2008 push.

It sits in the same years as the high-school bands: Rock I IV V, Casey McSocial, Mannequin, and Blue Suburbia. After this come The Other Side (2009) and Goodbye/Hello (2010).
