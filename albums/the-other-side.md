***The Other Side***: **2009** EP by **Luke Walton feat. Blue Suburbia** (~17:40 per [archived lukewaltonband.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20110714002356/http://lukewaltonband.com/site/CDs_and_Lyrics.html)). High-school **blues** band lineup, not **The Luke Walton Band** (USC cohort, 2009–2011).

Blue Suburbia was a blues trio at [La Costa Canyon High School](/music/la-costa-canyon/), about 2008 to 2009. Luke Walton (guitar, vocals), [Jordan Sorokin](/with/jordan-sorokin/) (bass), and [Zach Freeman](/with/zach-freeman/) (drums) co-founded it. It followed [Casey McSocial](/with/casey-mcsocial/), his first band, and Mannequin, which he also co-founded. They won the school's Battle of the Bands. The solo EPs [*Just A Friend*](/albums/just-a-friend/) and [*Heart in Hand*](/albums/heart-in-hand/) were going at the same time. The band's page is [here](/with/blue-suburbia/).

The [archived discography](https://web.archive.org/web/20110714002356/http://lukewaltonband.com/site/CDs_and_Lyrics.html) names that trio. A Wikipedia article on The Luke Walton Band listed the same roster. It was [deleted July 16, 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Articles_for_deletion/The_Luke_Walton_Band). The [June 18, 2026 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20260618142012/https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Luke_Walton_Band) is what remains. [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q15044350) does not name the members.

The songs are [Preschool](/songs/preschool/), a Walton original, [Move By Yourself](/songs/move-by-yourself/) (about 5:52, a Donavon Frankenreiter cover), [Let It Rock](/songs/let-it-rock/) (about 3:32, Kevin Rudolf featuring Lil Wayne), and [I Don't Need No Doctor](/songs/i-dont-need-no-doctor/) (about 4:46, a John Scofield and John Mayer arrangement of the Ray Charles song).

On August 20, 2009 they recorded at Soundboard Studio West in Rancho Bernardo. At the console: Jordan Sorokin, Zach Freeman, Luke Walton. Another frame is the three of them giving notes to the mix engineer. The engineer is not in the photo, and is not named.

A Preschool single was also listed on its own in 2009, at 3:40. The song later showed up on the June 2010 [Charitable Chords](https://charitablechords.bandcamp.com/album/know-your-rights-in-collaboration-with-knit-the-world) compilation as Luke Walton (Blue Suburbia), and again on [*Goodbye/Hello*](/albums/goodbye-hello/) in 2010.

Chronology: [*Just A Friend*](/albums/just-a-friend/) (2007) → [*Heart in Hand*](/albums/heart-in-hand/) (2008) → *The Other Side* (2009) → [*Goodbye/Hello*](/albums/goodbye-hello/) (2010).
