**Little Little Homes** is the 2016 FEiN EP that preceded the [*Little Homes*](/albums/little-homes-fein/) album (May 31, 2016). [Fresh Beats 365](https://freshbeats365.com/2016/03/10/fein-little-little-homes-ep-review/) reviewed it on **March 10, 2016**, so it was out by then. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/4vaKC2xgFmbjXYniF9Cl2v) still lists it: 2016, four songs, © 2016 Unsigned, ℗ 2016 Unsigned.

On **October 5, 2026**, the Spotify page showed **274,251** plays on [Sculptor](/songs/sculptor/) (3:55), **74,712** on [Girl You Can't Hide It (Album Version)](/songs/girl-you-cant-hide-it/) (3:23), **31,022** on [Outro](/songs/outro/) (1:27), and **3,954,072** on [#Grownupz (Album Version)](/songs/grownupz/) (3:42) ([screenshot](/evidence/fein-little-little-homes-spotify-2026-10-05.png)). The #Grownupz figure matches [FEiN Times (Issue #1)](/albums/fein-times-issue-1/). The Girl You Can't Hide It figure matches [FEiN Times (Issue #2)](/albums/fein-times-issue-2/). Those matching numbers are the counts on the pages, not extra plays to add together.
