Content

Wrap Music is a 15-track album by Burrito Bot, released June 14, 2024, on Beformer, about 23 minutes. Burrito Bot is a separate DistroKid artist from Scoobert Doobert, an AI burrito project. The cover was generated with Runway. Spotify spells the titles as released, including Tomatillo (Es Mi Perferida) and Guacamole Recipe (Receipe).

I Made 1000 AI Music Songs So You Don't Have to (July 2, 2024) is the project essay. It calls this the second LP, and it is the same day as the Love Music More episode. Refried Being is the first.
