FEiN Times (Issue #2) is the 2015 FEiN release between Issue #1 and Little Little Homes: Girl You Can't Hide It, Creatures, and Lonely People. Spotify files the three songs as a 2015 single, 11 minutes 28 seconds, © 2015 FEiN, ℗ 2015 FEiN. It is still up.

On October 5, 2026, that page showed 74,712 plays on Girl You Can't Hide It (3:26), 49,797 on Creatures (4:18), and 64,419 on Lonely People (3:44) (screenshot). Little Little Homes shows the same 74,712 figure on Girl You Can't Hide It (Album Version). That is one count on two pages, not a sum.
