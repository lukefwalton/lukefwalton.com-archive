## Record

### Photo archive

**Summary.** Every self-hosted photo of Luke F. Walton, Scoobert Doobert, and FEiN, newest first, grouped by year, with filters for the four core projects.

Photo archive at /gallery/. Default order is newest first. /gallery/oldest/ is oldest first. Undated photos stay at the end in both orders. Project filters: /gallery/scoobert-doobert/, /gallery/fein/, /gallery/luke-walton-band/, and /gallery/exist-elsewhere/. Each has an /oldest/ variant. Photos that are not one of those four projects still appear under All. FEiN and Scoobert Doobert each keep a short promo-and-stage sample on their own pages and link here. Licensed third-party photos stay as links on this page; they are not hosted here. Old bookmarks to /press-kit/photos/#logos and /press-kit/photos/#videos land on short notes here that point at /brand-kit/#logos and /press-kit/#videos.
