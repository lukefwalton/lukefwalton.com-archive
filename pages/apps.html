Apps: Luke F. Walton

Summary. Luke F. Walton has designed four apps: Surmado Scout (free, iOS and Android, Surmado's AI agent), Private Workout Logger ($4.99, iOS), Stop Political Spam Texts (free, MIT license, iOS), and A New Word Every Day (free, MIT license, iOS).

Apps hub at /apps/ lists every app Luke F. Walton designed. Surmado Scout: Surmado's AI agent, free to download on iPhone and Android; a paid Surmado membership is what makes it useful. App Store https://apps.apple.com/us/app/surmado-scout/id6756646583, Google Play https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.surmado.scout. Publisher: Surmado, Inc. He designed Scout (creator); it is a Surmado team product, so he is not credited as sole author. Private Workout Logger: privacy-first iPhone workout log, $4.99, source available. App Store https://apps.apple.com/us/app/private-workout-logger/id6782669888, source https://github.com/lukefwalton/workout-logger. On-device SQLite database only; no account, no server. Landing page /private-workout-logger/. Luke F. Walton designed and built it end to end (creator, author, publisher). Stop Political Spam Texts: free, MIT-licensed iPhone SMS filter. An on-device classifier routes campaign, fundraising, polling, and election texts from unknown senders to Junk. No account, no server, no political profiling. App Store https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stop-political-spam-texts/id6782703267, source https://github.com/lukefwalton/stop-political-texts. Landing page /stop-political-spam-texts/. Luke F. Walton designed and built it end to end (creator, author, publisher). A New Word Every Day: free, MIT-licensed iPhone vocabulary app and Home Screen widget. One rare, useful English word a day, no account, no server, no tracking. App Store https://apps.apple.com/us/app/a-new-word-every-day/id6785838096, source https://github.com/lukefwalton/a-new-word-every-day. Landing page /a-new-word-every-day/. Luke F. Walton designed and built it end to end (creator, author, publisher). Luke F.
