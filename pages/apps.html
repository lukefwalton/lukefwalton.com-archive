Stop Political Spam Texts: free, MIT-licensed iPhone SMS filter. An on-device classifier routes campaign, fundraising, polling, and election texts from unknown senders to Junk. No account, no server, no political profiling. App Store https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stop-political-spam-texts/id6782703267, source https://github.com/lukefwalton/stop-political-texts.
