### Answerability and Authored AI Systems

**Summary.** Three papers by Luke F. Walton on answerability and authorship. Answer Engine (technical note, repository, and live site) is set apart as a reference implementation.

Research hub at /research/ for Luke F. Walton's answerability work. There are three featured preprints so far, not four. They are preprints, not yet journal publications, with more under review. The Decision No One Authored: The Answerability Gap in Generative AI (Luke F. Walton; Preprint. Not yet peer-reviewed. PhilArchive WALTDN; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20614374; v2.0). Core claim, verbatim: "A person can retain full control of an AI-shaped decision and still fail to author it; the answerability gap is a missing act of judgment over the evaluative frame, not a missing participant or a missing capacity." The Captured Oracle: Authorship and Agency in the Ethics of Answer-Engine Optimization (Luke F. Walton; Preprint. Not yet peer-reviewed. PhilArchive WALTCO-87; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20676327). Core claim, verbatim: "When an answer engine settles a contested question in its own voice with no visible author, the wrong is false assurance — the appearance that someone answerable stands behind a frame whose author is hidden — and the remedy is answerable legibility." Pay Me What You Owe Me: On the Indefeasibility of the Demand for an Account (Luke F. Walton; Preprint. Not yet peer-reviewed. Zenodo; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23112498; v1.0). Core claim, verbatim: "Whenever an action reaches a party, an account is owed, and no routing of that action defeats the owing." Preprint v1.0, October 2, 2026. DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23112498. Concept DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20606492 resolves to the latest version. Its June 2026 working-paper version carried the earlier title The Invariant of Answerability (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20606493): the same paper under old language, not a separate work; that version is not withdrawn. Reading order: The Decision No One Authored (/writing/the-decision-no-one-authored/), then The Captured Oracle (/writing/the-captured-oracle/), then Pay Me What You Owe Me (/writing/pay-me-what-you-owe-me/). Set apart from the papers: Answer Engine technical note, open-source answer-engine repository, and Ask the Archive live deployment. One runnable shape of the argument, not a philosophical work.
