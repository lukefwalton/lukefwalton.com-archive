Luke F. Walton

Press

This is what has been written about Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert: interviews, commercial placements, production credits, and press coverage. A selection is below; radio and playlist logs and the full credit list follow further down.

  • He produced 「だれかおるやろ feat. CHAI」, the theme for Shingo Murakami’s series on NTT Docomo’s Lemino. Murakami sings and raps it as TAKATSU-KING. Walton mixed the song and played drums, bass, and guitar. The series premiered in April 2023. The song was never released on CD or streaming.
  • Rolling Stone Japan covered KOMAGOME's "WE CAN'T DOLL," which he produced and co-composed with MANA and KANA of CHAI.
  • BMI's Indie Spotlight profiled FEiN, the alternative-pop duo he formed with Brandon Woodward at USC.
  • Max Horwich wrote a chapter on Scoobert Doobert for Critical Meme Reader, a 2021 academic volume.
  • egoFM's ego 42 chart put "Don't Worry" at #1 on the Munich station's weekly chart in May 2021.
  • 91X's Top 91 of 2021 ranked "Don't Worry" #90.
  • Sound & Recording Magazine profiled his studio work, in Japan's recording and production magazine.
  • Pitchfork's WINK TOGETHER announcement lists his "Miracle" remix on the tracklist.
