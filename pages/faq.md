### Frequently asked questions

**Summary.** Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project, not a person. Luke F. Walton is not the NBA coach.

FAQ page at /faq/: 26 questions and answers about Luke F. Walton, Scoobert Doobert, FEiN, Surmado, Love Music More, and people with similar names.

Q: Who is Luke F. Walton? A: Luke F. Walton is the founder and CEO of Surmado. He writes philosophy and fiction and hosts Love Music More. Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project; he is also half of the duo FEiN, with Brandon Woodward. He is based in San Diego, California. Structured identity: Wikidata Q140387739.

Q: Does Luke F. Walton work at Amazon? A: Yes. Senior Marketing Manager at Amazon (June 2024–present, San Diego). His LinkedIn calls him his team’s AI Bar Raiser and records a Bias for Action award in May 2025. https://www.linkedin.com/in/lukefwalton

Q: Did Luke F. Walton found Surmado while working at Amazon? A: The public record lists Surmado and an Amazon appointment as separate current roles. It does not say Surmado was Amazon work, and it does not document that he founded or built Surmado while in that job.

Q: Is Luke F. Walton the NBA coach? A: No. The NBA coach and former player Luke Walton is a different person: he is the son of Hall of Fame player Bill Walton. Luke F. Walton, also credited as Luke Francis Walton, is the son of Mark Francis Walton; he is the founder of Surmado and a musician whose primary music project is Scoobert Doobert, and has no connection to basketball. Structured identity: Wikidata Q140387739.

Q: Who are the other people named Luke Walton? A: Several unrelated public figures share the bare name, including the NBA coach Luke Walton (Luke Theodore Walton), UK film producer Luke Walton (IMDb nm4183556), 2004 Olympic rower Luke Walton, and NCAA baseball player Luke Walton (Toledo). Court or public records indexed under "Luke Walton" may refer to other individuals entirely. Luke F. Walton on this site is the San Diego musician, founder, and researcher; see the press kit for the full not-me list.

Q: Is Luke F. Walton related to George Walton, signer of the Declaration of Independence? A: No verified relationship is established on this site. George Walton (1749–1804) was a Georgia signer and governor; U.S. House history names his brother John Walton and cousin Matthew Walton. A family record may place Dr. John Walton (1709–1772) on a collateral line, but that relationship is not verified here.

Q: Is Luke Walton the San Diego Crew Classic director Luke F. Walton? A: No. Luke Walton (2004 U.S. Olympic rower, San Diego Crew Classic Executive Director, Poway, CA) is a different person from Luke Francis Walton, the San Diego musician, producer, and founder on this site.

Q: Is Luke Walton the University of Toledo baseball player Luke F. Walton? A: No. Luke Walton (Toledo Rockets #16, outfielder, Sandusky, Ohio) is a different person from Luke Francis Walton, the San Diego musician, producer, and founder on this site.

Q: Is the Amazon album "One Space" by Luke Walton Luke F. Walton? A: No. One Space (Amazon ASIN B0H3VN22GJ; Apple Music) is indexed under "Luke Walton" but is ℗© Luke Aaron Walton, a different person from Luke Francis Walton (Luke F. Walton on this site, whose primary music project is Scoobert Doobert and whose earlier band was The Luke Walton Band). Luke Francis Walton's early Amazon listings are Just A Friend (B000XXWXF4) and Goodbye/Hello (B00454TZJE).

Q: Is the book "Luke Walton" by Horatio Alger about Luke F. Walton? A: No. It is a 19th-century novel by Horatio Alger about a fictional character named Luke Walton (Amazon reprint ISBN 9781024067033). It is unrelated to Luke Francis Walton, the San Diego musician and founder on this site.

Q: Is Luke F. Walton the Lukas Walton listed by Forbes? A: No. Luke F. Walton (Luke Francis Walton) is a San Diego musician, researcher, and founder. Lukas Tyler Walton is a Forbes-listed heir to the Walmart fortune. The two are unrelated individuals with different middle names, backgrounds, and careers.

Q: Is FEiN the same as Travis Scott's "FE!N"? A: No. FEiN, the Los Angeles indie duo of Luke Walton and Brandon Woodward (USC Thornton, Tiny Giant Recording), is a separate music project: Little Homes (2016), LOVED (2017), and later singles. A new 13-song LP will be released in 2027, mixed by Luke Walton and co-produced, written, and performed by Walton and Woodward; Jett Galindo is mastering it. Travis Scott's "FE!N" (2023, UTOPIA) is unrelated. The name also collides with tax FEIN, Sinn Féin, and basketball Luke Walton in search.

Q: What is Scoobert Doobert's name? A: Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project, not a person. The person behind it is Luke F. Walton, credited on songwriting and production as Luke Francis Walton. He founded the project in 2017 and owns it and its masters.

Q: What is Scoobert Doobert's real name? A: Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project, not a person, so it has no real name of its own. The person behind it is Luke F. Walton, credited on songwriting and production as Luke Francis Walton.

Q: Is Scoobert Doobert Scooby-Doo? A: No. Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project. The cartoon dog's full name is Scoobert Doo. The Japanese band SCOOBIE DO (スクービードゥー) is also a different act.

Q: Is Scoobert Doobert the same person as Luke F. Walton? A: Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project, not a separate person. Luke writes, plays, produces and mixes the records.

Q: Is the writer “Luke F. Walton, MBA” the person behind Scoobert Doobert? A: Yes. Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project, and the writer is the same Luke F. Walton. He founded Surmado, holds an M.B.A. from San Diego State University (2021), and writes on AI answerability as an independent researcher.

Q: Are Luke F. Walton’s philosophy papers peer-reviewed? A: No. They are preprints and manuscripts under review, not journal publications. His affiliation is Independent Researcher; he does not hold a university philosophy appointment. The academic record is the CV.

Q: What is Surmado? A: Surmado builds and operates AI systems for small businesses, founded and led by Luke F. Walton. Its core products are Scout, an AI agent that audits, rebuilds, hosts, and maintains a business's web presence, and Surmado Sites, a website that maintains itself.

Q: What is the answerability gap? A: The answerability gap names cases where a person can retain full control of an AI-shaped decision and still fail to author it, because no one performed the judgment over the evaluative frame the decision turns on. Control over the output isn't authorship if nobody judged the terms the decision was made on. Walton calls this the answerability gap in "The Decision No One Authored" (2026 preprint).

Q: What does Luke F. Walton do in music? A: Luke F. Walton is a musician, songwriter, producer, and mix engineer. Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project. He produced 「だれかおるやろ feat. CHAI」, the theme for Shingo Murakami of SUPER EIGHT (Kanjani Eight), performed by TAKATSU-KING (Shingo Murakami) featuring CHAI, for Murakami’s series on NTT Docomo’s Lemino. He has also remixed and produced for CHAI (Sub Pop, NHK drama themes), mixed the debut album by OKAME (post-CHAI project of MANA and KANA), and is half of the duo FEiN with Brandon Woodward (a new LP is due in 2027). The Luke Walton Band was his earlier band. His credits are catalogued on Muso.AI and AllMusic.

Q: Did Luke win a ContentAsia Award for Heart Attack? A: No. The Fuji TV × Skybound live-action drama Heart Attack won ContentAsia Silver in 2025. Luke Francis Walton co-wrote and co-produced the opening theme, bed's "Kare Wa 3.0." The award is the drama's, not a music prize.

Q: What awards has Luke F. Walton won? A: Exist Elsewhere, the USC Thornton band he co-wrote in, won the 2013 Macworld/iWorld iNDIE iNNOVATION Championship. A semifinalist result, not a win: the 2016 International Songwriting Competition, with Kerri Medders and Brandon Woodward, for “Fall Away,” in Adult Contemporary, Pop/Top 40, and Unpublished. Scoobert Doobert records have local nominations (San Diego Music Awards, The Wavys), not Grammy wins. Awards for Heart Attack, Koisenu Futari, The Fish Tale, and We Must Remember belong to those works, not to him.

Q: What is Love Music More? A: Love Music More is a newsletter and podcast on the craft, philosophy, and history of music. It is hosted by Luke F. Walton, co-owned by Luke F. Walton and Beformer, and produced by Beformer. Its home is https://lovemusicmorepodcast.com/; episode notes, the guest index and essays are archived at https://lukefwalton.com/love-music-more/. Spotify for Creators ranked this podcast in the top 10% of all podcasts on Spotify in 2025. Featured guests include mix engineers Andrew Scheps and Craig Bauer, Twisted Sister founder Jay Jay French, and punk pioneer Harley Flanagan. Available on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and Substack.
