<h4>applied communications</h4>
<p>Max Wood (Washington, D.C.), noisy screamy mostly electronic pop. Luke Francis Walton <strong>mixed</strong> the 2024 comeback EP <a href="../albums/applied-communications-has-a-midlife-crisis.html"><em>applied communications has a midlife crisis</em></a>, the singles after it, and the 2026 LP <a href="../albums/applied-communications-bites-the-big-one.html"><em>applied communications bites the big one</em></a> (mastered by Riley Knapp). <a href="https://daily.bandcamp.com/album-of-the-day/applied-communications-applied-communications-bites-the-big-one-review">Bandcamp Daily</a> named the LP Album of the Day on Sep 25, 2026. Earlier press: V13, Northern Transmissions, It's Psychedelic Baby, SF Shameless. <a href="https://applcomm.bandcamp.com/">Bandcamp</a>.</p>

### Catalog

**Summary.** Play counts on the catalog are a partial reading: Scoobert Doobert and FEiN on Spotify, plus the largest matched YouTube videos for those songs. Apple Music is not in the sum.

Play counts are a partial reading, added by counter, not by page. Scoobert Doobert on Spotify: 18,679,418. FEiN on Spotify: 23,861,526. YouTube, the largest matched videos for those songs: 4,656,478. Together that is 47,197,422. Apple Music is not in this sum, and neither is the rest of YouTube. Plays on other artists' releases, and his YouTube-only uploads, are separate: 8,285,127. SoundCloud is separate: 1,796,440. The numbers move. A blank Plays cell is a blank. The same figure on a single and an album is one count.
