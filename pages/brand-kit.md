<p>Self-hosted brand marks. SVG and PNG for web; EPS where available for print. The Surmado originals also live on <a href="https://www.surmado.com/">surmado.com</a>.</p>

<section>
<p>Colors · his personal brand colors</p>
<h4>1: Green (primary)</h4>
<p>The primary brand color, from the site homepage. Forest is the dark anchor; verdigris is the lighter shade for tints and accents.</p>
<h4>2: Blue (secondary)</h4>
<p>All the blues come from Japanese woodblock prints, not a generic web-blue: the Prussian blue (ベロ藍, <em>bero-ai</em>) and indigo (藍, <em>ai</em>) Hokusai used in <em>The Great Wave</em>.</p>
<h4>3: Accents</h4>
<p>Used sparingly: gold for calls to action, plus two rarer warm accents.</p>
<h4>Surfaces</h4>
<p>The website uses warm paper; the apps use cool paper over dark themes. Green ties the two together.</p>
<b>Web: warm</b> Rice-paper #F4EEDD · ink #111714.
<b>Apps: cool</b> Paper #EFF9FE over dark themes · ink #071D2B.
</section>

## Record

### Brand kit

**Summary.** Logos, personal brand colors, and type for Luke F. Walton: Scoobert Doobert, Love Music More, and Surmado marks, plus the forest-and-verdigris palette and the site typefaces.

Brand kit at /brand-kit/. Sections: Logos (#logos), Colors (#colors), Type (#type). The marks are the Scoobert Doobert SD monogram, the LOVE y? graphic, the Love Music More stacked wordmark (Grizzard Graphics), and the Surmado marks. Colors are his personal brand colors: forest and verdigris first, then the woodblock blues, then gold, rust, and jacaranda. Type is Fraunces, Literata, Inter, and Noto Serif JP, one face per purpose. /style/ redirects here. The #colors and #type anchors kept their names.
