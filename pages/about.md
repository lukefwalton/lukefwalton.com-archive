<h3>About</h3>
<p>🌐 English · <a href="jp.html">日本語でこのページを読む →</a></p>
<p>Luke F. Walton is the founder and CEO of <a href="https://www.surmado.com/">Surmado</a>. He writes <a href="research.html">philosophy</a> and <a href="https://lukefwalton.com/writing/#fiction">fiction</a> and hosts the <a href="love-music-more.html">Love Music More</a> podcast. <a href="music/scoobert-doobert.html">Scoobert Doobert</a> is Luke F. Walton's primary music project, an indie pop solo project. He is also half of <a href="music/fein.html">FEiN</a>, an alternative duo with Brandon Woodward. He is based in San Diego.</p>
<section>
<h4>Work</h4>
<p>He founded <a href="https://www.surmado.com/">Surmado</a>, which builds managed AI systems for small businesses. <a href="apps.html#surmado-scout">Surmado Scout</a>, an agent for the work most owners do not have time for, is on the App Store and Google Play.</p>
<p>He is Senior Marketing Manager at Amazon (June 2024–present), on global paid media for seller-facing programs. From 2018 to 2024 he worked at <a href="https://www.namm.org/">NAMM</a>, ending as Associate Director of Marketing. The Associate Director years included international marketing for Asia Pacific, with an emphasis on Japan. The fuller corporate record and earlier appointments are on the <a href="cv.html">CV</a>.</p>
</section>
<section>
<h4>Research</h4>
<p>Walton's current research focuses on authorship and answerability in automated systems. <a href="../writing/the-decision-no-one-authored.html">“The Decision No One Authored: The Answerability Gap in Generative AI”</a> (2026), <a href="../writing/the-captured-oracle.html">“The Captured Oracle”</a>, and <a href="../writing/pay-me-what-you-owe-me.html">“Pay Me What You Owe Me”</a> (October 2026) are available as preprints under <a href="research.html">Research</a>. Publication status for these and his other papers is on the <a href="cv.html">CV</a>.</p>
</section>
<section>
<h4>Music</h4>
<p>Luke F. Walton has been commercially releasing music since 2007. His most notable works are released by <a href="music/scoobert-doobert.html">Scoobert Doobert</a>, his primary music project, and <a href="music/fein.html">FEiN</a>, his duo with Brandon Woodward. FEiN started in 2014. He founded Scoobert Doobert in 2017 and owns it and its masters. In 2014 and 2015 he played guitar for <a href="with/lara-johnston.html">Lara Johnston</a> on tours supporting <a href="with/the-doobie-brothers.html">The Doobie Brothers</a> and <a href="with/gregg-allman.html">Gregg Allman</a>.</p>
<p>On February 13, 2016, FEiN’s “<a href="../songs/grownupz.html">#Grownupz</a>” hit #4 on Spotify’s US Viral 50. “<a href="../songs/loved.html">LOVED</a>” has more than 9 million Spotify streams. For Scoobert Doobert he has written, recorded, arranged, produced, and mixed more than 250 songs. “<a href="../songs/im-an-idiot.html">I’m an Idiot</a>” was on Spotify’s global New Music Friday on September 3, 2021, where MANA and KANA of <a href="with/chai.html">CHAI</a> discovered him. He produced CHAI’s “<a href="../songs/chai-whole.html">WHOLE</a>,” the theme for the NHK drama <em>Koisenu Futari</em>, and <a href="../songs/chai-dareka-oru-yaro.html">「だれかおるやろ feat. CHAI」</a> for <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shingo_Murakami">Shingo Murakami</a> of SUPER EIGHT. He co-wrote and co-produced bed’s “<a href="../songs/bed-kare-wa.html">Kare Wa 3.0</a>,” the opening theme for the Fuji TV drama <em>Heart Attack</em>, and mixed <a href="../albums/okame-debut.html">OKAME’s 2026 debut album</a>. The records are on <a href="music.html">Music</a>.</p>

### About: music and philosophy

**Summary.** Luke F. Walton's music, writing, and research share a public record: the Horwich chapter, The Bent Stick, Love Music More, and the answerability papers.

How are Luke's music and philosophy connected? The Horwich chapter Masks Monsters and Memes (Critical Meme Reader 2021) puts Scoobert Doobert on record: Socrates, absurdism, memetic production, persona versus person. The Bent Stick essay connects lived experience to authorship, language, and standing behind what you cannot prove. Love Music More is a podcast and newsletter on the craft, history, and philosophy of music. Interviews include Andrew Scheps, Craig Bauer, Phillip Broussard Jr., Alex Delicata, David Baron, Travis Ference, Bermuda Schwartz, Jay Jay French, and Harley Flanagan. Solo episodes include a History of Rock Music series. The Getty Degas doppelgänger is a separate folklore page, not part of the show. The Decision No One Authored, The Captured Oracle, and Pay Me What You Owe Me are papers on authorship and answerability in AI ethics.
