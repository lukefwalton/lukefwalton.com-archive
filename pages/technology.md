### Technology: Luke F. Walton

**Summary.** The Technology page for Luke F. Walton: Surmado, which builds managed AI systems for small businesses; Answer Engine, an open reference implementation of citation-grounded AI answers; and the apps he designed.

Technology hub at /technology/. Luke F. Walton founded Surmado, a technology company for small business. Surmado builds managed AI systems for small businesses. Surmado Scout, an agent for the work most owners do not have time for, is on the App Store and Google Play. Answer Engine is a small, open reference implementation of citation-grounded AI answers, built for engineers who want the design contract running in code. It runs Ask the Archive on lukefwalton.com (/ask/about/); the retrieval API is at /ask/api/. Apps he designed, listed at /apps/: Surmado Scout, Stop Political Spam Texts, A New Word Every Day, Convert Recipe to Grocery List. Founder letters, essays on building Surmado, are at /letters/.
