Identity

Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project. He founded it in 2017 and owns it and its masters; Beformer is the label (some releases are co-signed Beformer × indiemono), and he records at Micasa Studios, his private San Diego studio. It is self-produced indie and chill pop: he writes, plays, produces, and mixes the records himself, credited on songwriting and production as Luke Francis Walton. The artist site is scoobertdoobert.pizza. The wider story (FEiN, NAMM, Love Music More, and the production work) is on Music.

Where to start

Three songs that show what the catalog sounds like.

  • I'm an Idiot A Big Hug single that turns self-deprecation into relief, from missing every social cue to realizing nobody is watching.
  • Don't Worry A Big Hug track that plays anxiety for satire, a 2 pm breakdown and worrying about not living in the now.
  • Miracle (Scoobert Doobert Remix) A remix for CHAI, track 4 on Sub Pop's WINK TOGETHER (2022). The CHAI production credits, "WHOLE" and "MY DREAM," came after it.
