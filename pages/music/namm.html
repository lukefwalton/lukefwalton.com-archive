NAMM

. Walton first fell in love with the local music store at American Music Store in Encinitas, where he studied guitar with Jeff Moore as a child. Bud Whitcomb is the founder and former master guitar technician of American Music Exchange (AMX Music), with over 40 years of experience in instrument repair and setup in the San Diego/Encinitas area. His first day on NAMM staff was Friday, November 2, 2018. A lobby screen read “NAMM & MoMM welcome Luke Walton.” MoMM is the Museum of Making Music, at NAMM headquarters in Carlsbad.

The job

From 2018 to 2024 Walton worked inside NAMM’s music-products and pro-audio world, eventually as Associate Director of Marketing for The NAMM Show. He kept touring, recording, and mixing music the whole time, and used that vantage point to evaluate gear and interview the people who make and use it, for trade and public audiences.

By his account, he first reported to Andy Tompkins, who reported to Joe Lamond, then NAMM’s CEO. Later he reported to Pete Johnston, who reported to John Mlynczak, NAMM’s President and CEO.
