### Thornton School of Music

**Summary.** The Thornton School of Music is the music school at the University of Southern California in Los Angeles. The school’s full name is the USC Thornton School of Music. Luke F. Walton graduated from it in 2013 with a B.M. in Popular Music. He was in the inaugural class of that program and entered in 2009.
