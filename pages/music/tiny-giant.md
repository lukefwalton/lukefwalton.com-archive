### Tiny Giant Recording

**Summary.** Tiny Giant Recording is the recording studio in Westlake Village, California, that Luke F. Walton and Brandon Woodward co-own. FEiN recorded there, and so did the client sessions from those years. Scoobert Doobert does not record there.
