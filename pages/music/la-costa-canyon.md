### La Costa Canyon High School

**Summary.** La Costa Canyon High School is where Luke F. Walton finished high school. He graduated in January 2009, a semester early. The bands from those years were Casey McSocial, then Mannequin, then Blue Suburbia, alongside the solo EPs Just A Friend and Heart in Hand.
