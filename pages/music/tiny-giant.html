Tiny Giant Recording

Summary. Tiny Giant Recording is the recording studio in Westlake Village, California, that Luke F. Walton and Brandon Woodward co-own. FEiN recorded there, and so did the client sessions from those years. Scoobert Doobert does not record there.

Tiny Giant Recording is the recording studio in Westlake Village, California, that Luke F. Walton and Brandon Woodward co-own. FEiN recorded there, and so did the client sessions from those years. Scoobert Doobert does not record there. Tiny Giant Recording, the Westlake Village studio Luke F. Walton and Brandon Woodward co-own The studio site is https://tinygiantrecording.com/.
