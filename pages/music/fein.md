## Record

### FEiN

**Summary.** FEiN is the indie duo of Luke Walton and Brandon Woodward; separate from Scoobert Doobert solo and from Travis Scott FE!N.

Luke Walton and Brandon Woodward recorded as FEiN: Little Homes (2016), LOVED (2017), and later singles. FEiN's new 13-song LP will be released in 2027. It is still unreleased. Mixed by Luke Francis Walton, and co-produced, written, and performed by Luke Francis Walton and Brandon Michael Woodward. Jett Galindo is mastering it. She is a Grammy-winning mastering engineer with credits including Billie Eilish, Green Day, Pink Floyd, and Elvis Costello. That Grammy is hers, not a Luke F. Walton award and not a FEiN award.
