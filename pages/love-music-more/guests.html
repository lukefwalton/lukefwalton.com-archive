Love Music More: podcast guests

Summary. Guest hub for the Love Music More podcast, hosted by Luke F. Walton.

Love Music More: newsletter and podcast hosted by Luke F. Walton on the craft, philosophy, and history of music. Top 10% of all podcasts on Spotify. Guests include Andrew Scheps, Craig Bauer, Andy Shernoff. New episodes Tuesdays. The guest hub at /love-music-more/guests/ lists each interview guest with the full episode and, where one exists, a Love Music Why clip. Guests also include Harley Flanagan (Cro-Mags), Jay Jay French (Twisted Sister), and Bubby Lewis (bassist, Snoop Dogg, Dr. Dre, Stevie Wonder).
