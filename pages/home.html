Luke F. Walton founded Surmado, a technology company for small business. He is a multi-instrumentalist and produces, mixes, and arranges for artists such as Shingo Murakami, CHAI, bed, and OKAME. Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project; he is also half of the duo FEiN. He hosts the Love Music More podcast, with guests such as Andrew Scheps (Adele) and Craig Bauer (Kanye West). He also writes philosophy, fiction, and essays. Read more on his biography.
