Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Victor Marc. Status on site: complete. Victor Marc is a songwriter and singer from Aix-les-Bains. He is self-taught and self-produced, and live he is a one-man band with a looper: electro-pop, folk, and older rock (his about page, Festival Décapadiot, Les Puristes). The debut album, Mood Swings, came out October 3, 2025 on Monty People, with songs in English and in French (Café de la Danse). The vinyl campaigns run through Diggers Factory, per his releases page. The dated shows: Musilac Tremplin in 2022, the Korner stage in Aix-les-Bains (Le Dauphiné). Support for James Blunt at L'Arcadium in Annecy on July 25, 2024 (setlist.fm).
