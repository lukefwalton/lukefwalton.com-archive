Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Nick Campbell. Status on site: complete. Also credited as: Nick "Thor" Campbell. Nick Campbell played bass in The Luke Walton Band on Goodbye/Hello, out September 1, 2010. Logan Shrewsbury played drums, Peter Lee Johnson violin, and Luke guitar and voice. In college Luke called him Thor. The nickname stayed on the LP credits and in later interviews, including Growing as an Artist. The Bear Attack Bandcamp about line also names Campbell and Shrewsbury, with Eric Radloff, Mia Minichiello, and Brandon Bae. Nick Campbell is connected to the Thornton School of Music, which Luke F. Walton graduated from. That class played weekly shows in Los Angeles. On the 2010 California coast tour, San Diego to San Francisco, Luke and Rozzi Crane booked their first tour together. The Luke Walton Band played one bill. Crane's band was the same Walton lineup with Sam Wilkes on bass. Campbell played bass on the LP and the showcase clips, and not on that road leg. Popular Music Showcase 2010 footage has Crane singing, Walton on guitar, and Campbell on bass, on covers of Toto and Jill Scott. He came back for the Justin Klunk EP, July 19, 2013. Klunk played sax, Gabe Rudner keys, Campbell bass, Walton guitar, and Nate Laguzza drums. He is a session bassist and composer in Los Angeles. SoundBetter and La Bella Strings list work with Pomplamoose, Scary Pockets, Vulfpeck, Charlie Puth, Meghan Trainor, Nigel Hall, Moonchild, and others. As Nick Campbell Destroys he puts out industrial jazz-funk: Art (December 2022) and Live for the Highest Bidder (July 2023). He teaches on Scott's Bass Lessons. The Luke Walton Band: Goodbye/Hello (Sep 2010 LP · Campbell bass · Walton guitar · Shrewsbury drums · Peter Lee Johnson violin); Rosanna (Toto cover): Popular Music Showcase 2010 (Campbell bass · Logan Shrewsbury drums · Walton guitar · Rozzi Crane vocals · /music/#selected-videos); A Long Walk (Jill Scott cover) (Campbell bass · Logan Shrewsbury drums · Walton guitar · Crane vocals · student showcase).
