Patrice Rushen is a Grammy-nominated pianist, composer, arranger, and music director. Her 1982 "Forget Me Nots" became the bassline for Will Smith's "Men in Black" (1997). She was the first woman to serve as musical director for the Grammy Awards, the Emmy Awards, and the NAACP Image Awards. From 2013 to 2023 she chaired USC Thornton's Popular Music Program (USC Thornton, NEA Jazz Masters). Luke studied music direction with Patrice Rushen at USC Thornton.
