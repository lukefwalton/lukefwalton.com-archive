### With Garvie

**Summary.** London artist. Luke mixed six of his singles from 2022 to 2024. Garvie produced them. Decisions Decisions reached BBC Radio 1's Next Wave.

London artist. Luke mixed six of his singles from 2022 to 2024. Garvie produced them. Decisions Decisions reached BBC Radio 1's Next Wave. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Garvie. Status on site: complete. Garvie makes alt-pop in London, mostly on his own, with a computer-and-N64 sound. Aipate places him there. The releases run from Villain in 2021 through the Boring EP in 2024. Luke's job on the run was the mix. He did not produce the records. Decisions Decisions, a Scoobert mix, reached BBC Radio 1's Next Wave with Jack Saunders. Garvie's Instagram from November 23, 2022 is the receipt, and the release-day reel from October 14 tags the mix. On So High, Melodic Magazine and The Pentatonic name Scoobert Doobert's mix and Garvie's production. The other mixes are Default, Real Life, Never Learn, and Boring. Nothing of his has come out since Boring.
