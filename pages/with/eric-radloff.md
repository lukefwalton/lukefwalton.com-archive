### With Eric Radloff

**Summary.** Eric Radloff records as Okudaxij (earlier Scoobert credits: Gokudaxij; Bear Attack credits sometimes spell Erik). Bear Attack is his band. Featured vocalist and co-writer with Scoobert Doobert, including Happy Birthday and the Babidi & Gokudaxij tracks. Babidi on those tracks is Taylor Washington (J MESA), a different person. Artist site: okudaxij.bandcamp.com.
