With Bubby Lewis

Summary. Session and touring bassist. Bass on Scoobert Doobert’s Tired of the Sunshine (Moonlight Beach, 2023), singing for u, it gets better, and golden state of mind on I (2024), chasing the sunset (2024), and LOL and THIS IS FINE (2025). Love Music More guest, September 12, 2023.

Session and touring bassist. Bass on Scoobert Doobert’s Tired of the Sunshine (Moonlight Beach, 2023), singing for u, it gets better, and golden state of mind on I (2024), chasing the sunset (2024), and LOL and THIS IS FINE (2025). Love Music More guest, September 12, 2023. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Bubby Lewis. Status on site: complete. Bubby Lewis plays bass on Scoobert Doobert records. Tired of the Sunshine on Moonlight Beach (2023) bills him as the featured player, and the DistroKid credit pane lists him on bass. The I liner notes (June 14, 2024) name him on bass for tracks 5, 12, and 13, singing for u, it gets better, and golden state of mind, on an MTD Bubby Lewis Signature. The DistroKid credit panes for chasing the sunset (August 9, 2024), LOL, and THIS IS FINE (2025) list him on bass. Love Music More: Bass, Japan, and Videogame Music (September 12, 2023). That episode is his account of the wider session career. This page stays on the Scoobert credits.
