With Garvie

Summary. London alt-pop / pop-rock artist (self-produced DIY, computer/N64-ish aesthetic). Luke Francis Walton mixed six Garvie singles (2022–2024); Garvie self-produced: Decisions Decisions hit BBC Radio 1 Next Wave (Jack Saunders); Melodic Magazine and The Pentatonic cite Scoobert’s mix on “So High.”

London alt-pop / pop-rock artist (self-produced DIY, computer/N64-ish aesthetic). Luke Francis Walton mixed six Garvie singles (2022–2024); Garvie self-produced: Decisions Decisions hit BBC Radio 1 Next Wave (Jack Saunders); Melodic Magazine and The Pentatonic cite Scoobert’s mix on “So High.” Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Garvie. Status on site: complete. Garvie is a London artist making alt-pop and pop-rock with a self-produced, computer/N64-ish aesthetic. Aipate calls him a London artist; Apple Music shows the release trail from “Villain” (2021) through the 2024 Boring EP. Walton’s role across the run is mix only, not produce. Decisions Decisions, a Scoobert mix, reached BBC Radio 1 Next Wave / Future Artists with Jack Saunders (@garviesounds Nov 23, 2022). @garviesounds tagged @scoobertdoobert.pizza for the mix on release day (Oct 14, 2022). Public press on So High: Garvie self-produced; Melodic Magazine and The Pentatonic name Scoobert Doobert’s mix. Walton also mixed Default, Real Life, Never Learn, and Boring. No Garvie releases since Boring (2024). Mixed: Decisions Decisions (2022 · BBC R1 Next Wave · @garviesounds mix credit · York Calling); Never Learn (2023); Real Life (2023); Default (2023 · Aipate); So High (2023 · Melodic Mag, Pentatonic); Boring (2024).
