With Hammer City

Summary. Hammer City / The Hammers: Nick Belcher's Los Angeles rock project. Heartquake Saga EP (Aug 31, 2016): 4 songs, ~18 min, ℗ Hammer City. Luke Francis Walton recording engineer; public materials tie Tiny Giant Recording and Frank Rosato mixing. Walton did not write the songs.
