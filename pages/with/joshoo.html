Trumpeter, horn arranger, and multi-instrumentalist (Josh Shpak / Joshoo). Public credits include trumpet on Peter Gabriel's i/o era: Road to Joy and Olive Tree, plus membership in Gabriel's 2023 i/o The Tour band. Former Ripe trumpet player. Co-writer with Luke Francis Walton on Ok Ok OK. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Joshoo. Status on site: complete. Also credited as: Josh Shpak. Josh Shpak records and tours as Joshoo. He trained at Berklee, plays trumpet, arranges horns, and produces. He studied with Clark Terry. Yamaha has an artist page. Before the Peter Gabriel work he played trumpet in Ripe, the Berklee band that started in Boston and moved toward Los Angeles. He and Calvin Barthel were the horns, trumpet and trombone, from the 2011 lineup, per Wikipedia) and Discogs. The Gabriel credits are public. He joined i/o The Tour in 2023 on trumpet, French horn, EWI, keyboards, and vocals. Gabriel's engineer, Oli Jacobs, heard him rehearsing next door and recommended him (At The Barrier, PeterGabriel.com). On the album he plays trumpet on Road to Joy and Olive Tree (Pitchfork, Wikipedia)). The Olive Tree session was home-studio trumpet overdubs in the summer of 2022, stacked until it sounded like a section. With Scoobert Doobert he co-wrote Ok Ok OK in 2023, Joshoo and Scoobert Doobert. Streaming metadata names Josh Shpak and Luke Francis Walton. He talked about touring, sync, and Apple commercials on Love Music More. Co-writing: Ok Ok OK (2023 · Joshoo × Scoobert Doobert · Shpak + Walton writers).
