# With Blue Suburbia

## Record

### With Blue Suburbia

**Summary.** La Costa Canyon high-school classic rock / blues trio (~2008–2009), co-founded as equals by Luke Walton (guitar, vocals), Zach Freeman (drums), and Jordan Sorokin (bass). Won LCC Battle of the Bands; recorded The Other Side (2009).

La Costa Canyon high-school classic rock / blues trio (~2008–2009), co-founded as equals by Luke Walton (guitar, vocals), Zach Freeman (drums), and Jordan Sorokin (bass). Won LCC Battle of the Bands; recorded The Other Side (2009). Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Blue Suburbia. Status on site: complete. Blue Suburbia followed Mannequin in Walton's La Costa Canyon bands: Casey McSocial (first band), then Mannequin (Walton co-founded), then Blue Suburbia, then The Luke Walton Band. Solo EPs Just A Friend and Heart in Hand ran in parallel with all three bands. The group was a cover-forward blues trio, playing Eric Clapton, John Mayer, and similar, with a few Walton originals, chiefly Preschool. Sources: the archived lukewaltonband.com discography names Luke Walton, Zach Freeman (drums), and Jordan Sorokin (bass) as Blue Suburbia; the band won La Costa Canyon's Battle of the Bands and recorded the four-song EP The Other Side (17:40, summer 2009) before members left for college. The same roster was on the English Wikipedia article for The Luke Walton Band, deleted July 16, 2026 (Wayback capture, June 18, 2026); the surviving structured record, Wikidata Q15044350, carries no lineup. The 2009 Coast News profile confirms Walton's LCC Battle of the Bands results (Blue Suburbia win). The archived discography lists the release as Luke Walton feat. Blue Suburbia. Blue Suburbia closed the La Costa Canyon Battle of the Bands run: Walton did not compete freshman year, won with Casey McSocial, placed second with Mannequin, then won again here. The Coast News summarized this as winning all but one year. Round by round, with sources: Catalog → LCC Battle of the Bands. Member hubs: Jordan Sorokin, Ph.D. · Zach Freeman. Releases: Soundboard Studio West (August 20, 2009 · Rancho Bernardo · left to right: Jordan Sorokin, Zach Freeman, Luke Walton, giving notes to the mix engineer · the engineer is not in the frame and is not named); The Other Side (EP) (2009 · 17:40 · Luke Walton feat. Blue Suburbia · Preschool · three covers); Preschool (single) (2009 · 3:40 · also on Charitable Chords comp (Jun 2010, Luke Walton feat. Blue Suburbia)). Press on Blue Suburbia releases: Wikidata: The Luke Walton Band (Q15044350): replaces the English Wikipedia article, deleted Jul 16, 2026, Wayback: The Luke Walton Band (English Wikipedia, captured Jun 18, 2026, deleted Jul 16), The Coast News: Six Months to Make It (2009), Luke Walton Band: CDs & Lyrics (archived), Charitable Chords: Know Your Rights ("Luke Walton (Blue Suburbia) — Preschool"). Related hubs: Jordan Sorokin, Ph.D., Zach Freeman, Casey McSocial (prior LCC chapter), Catalog: LCC Battle of the Bands, round by round.
