With bed

Summary. Luke Francis Walton remixed mother ship and co-wrote, co-produced, and mixed Kare Wa 3.0 with Tokyo underground rave-rock band bed, opening theme for Fuji TV × Skybound live-action drama HEART ATTACK. bed formed in 2022 (UNE, SINJI, JONEU, SHUN-1) and built a following through Tokyo after-midnight club sets and warehouse parties before recorded releases went international.

Luke Francis Walton remixed mother ship and co-wrote, co-produced, and mixed Kare Wa 3.0 with Tokyo underground rave-rock band bed, opening theme for Fuji TV × Skybound live-action drama HEART ATTACK. bed formed in 2022 (UNE, SINJI, JONEU, SHUN-1) and built a following through Tokyo after-midnight club sets and warehouse parties before recorded releases went international. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with bed. Status on site: complete. bed are post-punk / electronic-experimentation / rave-rock. GATA and Crack describe Tokyo after-midnight sets: metal-machine basslines, big riffs, techno kicks, smoke and strobes, songs flowing like a techno mix. They ran bedroom parties at Shibuya clubasia (band + house/techno DJs) and reportedly filled a 600-person Shin-Kiba warehouse in 2023. Crack describes bed as an ongoing 2052 world-building project: blacked-out visual identity, Matrix-green flyers, cyberpunk styling, music/artwork/stage as one universe. Singles from July 2022 (APOLOGIZE) through Kare Wa, Michael Mann, mother ship (April 2023), 130, ISEI – Live. FRIENDSHIP. catalogs release dates; Archives : May 27th, 2023 was their first album-length drop, but EYESCREAM quotes the band refusing a “1st album” made to tour, play festivals, or satisfy media: a live-recording archive instead. On HEART ATTACK, Japanese press (Spincoaster, SENSA) says director Takeshi Maruyama first imagined opening the Fuji TV drama with the existing live Kare Wa 2.0; bed then proposed a newly recorded 3.0 as the definitive TV version. In September 2025 Fuji TV announced that the live-action series won ContentAsia Silver for Best Drama Series Made in Asia for a Regional or International Market: the award is the drama’s, not a music prize. Internationally, Crack reports a 2025 London show leading to Forty Miles and 2026 UK dates (Windmill, George Tavern, LOST). Remixes: mother ship (Scoobert Doobert Remix) (April 2023 single · after APOLOGIZE, Kare Wa, and Michael Mann). Mixing / production: Kare Wa 3.0 (HEART ATTACK OP · Maruyama wanted live 2.0 energy; bed recorded TV version · co-write, co-produce, mix). Press on bed releases: SENSA: Kare Wa 3.0 / HEART ATTACK, Fuji TV: HEART ATTACK ContentAsia Silver (2025), Sources: Fuji TV ContentAsia Silver, Spincoaster: Kare Wa 3.0, SENSA: Archives live album, EYESCREAM: Archives : May 27th, 2023, Crack: Tokyo live phenomenon / Forty Miles, GATA: post-punk renegades, Japan Times, Kare Wa 3.0 (official MV), Kare Wa 3.0: production demo synced to HEART ATTACK OP, FRIENDSHIP.: artist page.
