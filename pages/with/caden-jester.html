Summary. Singer-songwriter. Let You Go (feat. FEiN) plays in The Fosters S5 E9. Luke: guitar, including the solo, and songwriting.

Singer-songwriter. Let You Go (feat. FEiN) plays in The Fosters S5 E9. Luke: guitar, including the solo, and songwriting. Collaborator hub for Luke F.
