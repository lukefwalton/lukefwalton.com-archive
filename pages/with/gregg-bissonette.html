Berklee lists him as a session drummer, a touring drummer, and a teacher. This page is the thread to Luke and FEiN. He taught Brandon Woodward drums in high school. Woodward later co-wrote, co-produced, and traded vocals with Luke on Little Homes at Tiny Giant. Discogs thanks Bissonette on that LP, next to Frank Rosato. Thanks is not a performance. He does not play on Little Homes. On Jim Camacho's Looking for a Devil Among Angels he does play. Frank Rosato recorded the drums at Woodcliff, on a Fernando Perdomo production.
