# With Chan Fuze

## Record

### With Chan Fuze

**Summary.** Chicago alt-rock / alt-R&B artist (Chandler Scott Fuzak). Luke Francis Walton mixed This Is Not Cool and Methadone Nights (2023); Tommaso Veronesi produced both.

Chicago alt-rock / alt-R&B artist (Chandler Scott Fuzak). Luke Francis Walton mixed This Is Not Cool and Methadone Nights (2023); Tommaso Veronesi produced both. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Chan Fuze. Status on site: complete. Chan Fuze blends alternative rock and alternative R&B from Chicago. Reggies Chicago cites Rolling Stones, Tame Impala, and Talking Heads in his sound; Apple Music lists releases including Can I See U L8r?, Get Real, and 2025 album Boyhood. Walton’s credit on the 2023 pair is mix only. Tommaso Veronesi (artist project TOMMASO; also Tommy Veronesi in Chicago teaching contexts) produced: UnitedMasters composer credits list Fuzak / Veronesi. Veronesi also released the 2022 While Away collab with Scoobert Doobert as TOMMASO. Mixed: This Is Not Cool (2023 · prod. Tommaso Veronesi · Marmoset staff pick); Methadone Nights (2023 · prod. Tommaso Veronesi). Press on Chan Fuze releases: Marmoset: This Is Not Cool, WGN-TV live, York Calling: Sugar. Related hubs: With Tommaso Veronesi, Love Music More: TOMMASO.
