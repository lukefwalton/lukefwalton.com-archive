With Petite Meller

Summary. French-Israeli singer-songwriter (Island Records; debut Lil Empire, 2016). Luke Walton hosted a Tiny Giant session on June 22, 2018, working on unreleased songs with Petite Meller. The photo is hers: she is in the mirror. Nothing from that session was released.

French-Israeli singer-songwriter (Island Records; debut Lil Empire, 2016). Luke Walton hosted a Tiny Giant session on June 22, 2018, working on unreleased songs with Petite Meller. Petite Meller (Syvan Meller) is a French-Israeli pop artist between Paris, Tel Aviv, and Los Angeles; see Wikipedia. Former Terry Poison vocalist; Baby Love (2015) and debut album Lil Empire (Sept 2016) were recorded across Brixton, Stockholm, and LA. Luke's connection is a Tiny Giant studio session on June 22, 2018, documented by a behind-the-scenes photo credited to Petite Meller: she appears in the mirror taking the shot while Luke is at the desk. The post is location-tagged Westlake Village. Luke and Petite worked on songs in that session; nothing from it was released. The photo is the main documentation of the session.
