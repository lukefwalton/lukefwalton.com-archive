Tamtam, Reem Altamimi, is a Saudi singer-songwriter who works between Riyadh and Los Angeles. The WiMN and her own bio talk about empowerment and a bridge between places. Vogue Arabia profiled her in June 2025, in Saudis Abroad. That piece is about her, not a credit with Luke. FEKR writes up Rise (2018) as a song she wrote with Jordan Benjamin, Grandson, "produced by Brandon Woodward and Luke Walton, commonly known as Fein," with a video by Meshal Al Jaser. She calls it a favorite because a lot of her favorite creatives were on it. In a What's On interview in December 2021 she names Luke and Brandon as producers and says she hopes to keep working with FEiN: "they are in a band called Fein." The same piece calls Kuwait Rising an amazing festival where the crowd sang along. Kuwait Rising was April 27, 2018, at the DAI cultural center. Red Bull has Joss Stone headlining and Tamtam on the bill. Luke played with her. The Lyn Winter press release for Rise says so. Arab News credits Drive to her lyrics, production by Siren, and Luke as executive producer, around the lifting of the ban on women driving. ROLLERCOASTER (2023) was produced and played by Luke and Brandon. His SoundBetter profile also lists guitar for her. Those production credits sit after the Doobie Brothers and Gregg Allman years with Lara Johnston's band, and before the Scoobert bedroom-pop records. Accidental Muse, in 2018, is the side project. Summertime Feeling and five later singles are billed ax and Tamtam on Spotify. ax is Luke. She sang on every one of them, including Summertime Feeling. That is his account. Glamglare (July 12, 2018) calls Summertime Feeling the debut and names neither of them. It describes a hidden Los Angeles persona who toured as a hired guitarist for the Doobie Brothers, Gregg Allman, and Heart. The Doobie and Allman lines match the Lara Johnston dates. Heart is only in that piece. On the run he was composer, co-writer, recording engineer, and mix engineer, on keys and guitar. Same year as Drive and Rise. Distro metadata on his own Friends, from the $WAMI$ era, also used the Accidental Muse name. That is a separate use of the name. Later Tamtam releases outside this list are not his. The 2022 Coke Studio FIFA World Cup rework of A Kind of Magic, with Felukah, Tamtam, and Danna Paola, does not involve him. His credits with her run 2018 to 2023: production, Accidental Muse, and live dates.
