Malik LovesYall co-wrote All in the Feeling with Luke Walton and performs on it, and has the verse on Kick It in Nirvana, also released as a standalone single in October 2021. Both are on Big Hug (October 8, 2021). He performs with J MESA on Live from the Void, including My Mind Is Slowly Slipping (Live).
