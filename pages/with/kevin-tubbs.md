# With Kevin Tubbs

## Record

### With Kevin Tubbs

**Summary.** Records as Sanguine Penguin (@ktubbles). Former artist name KAJAX. Three Japan-facing collab singles with Scoobert Doobert (2022–2023): Bumblebae and do what i wanna were originally released as KAJAX and re-released as Sanguine Penguin; wowowow is billed Sanguine Penguin.

Records as Sanguine Penguin (@ktubbles). Former artist name KAJAX. Three Japan-facing collab singles with Scoobert Doobert (2022–2023): Bumblebae and do what i wanna were originally released as KAJAX and re-released as Sanguine Penguin; wowowow is billed Sanguine Penguin. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Kevin Tubbs. Status on site: complete. Also credited as: Sanguine Penguin, K Tubbles, KAJAX, Kajax. Kevin Tubbs is the person behind the Sanguine Penguin release name on streaming (@ktubbles). KAJAX (also written Kajax) was his former artist name. Three standalone Japan-facing singles with Scoobert Doobert (2022–Apr 2023): Bumblebae, do what i wanna, and wowowow. Luke Francis Walton on the Scoobert side. Bumblebae and do what i wanna were originally released as KAJAX × Scoobert Doobert, then re-released as Sanguine Penguin. SoundExchange still lists the Kajax credit on the Bumblebae ISRC; Spotify now indexes Sanguine Penguin. Same person, not two artists. wowowow (2023) is billed Sanguine Penguin. @ktubbles bass promo ahead of the Apr 15, 2022 Spotify drop; @beformer confirmed official OFF POP playlist add the same day. Collaborations with Scoobert Doobert: Bumblebae (Apr 15, 2022 · originally KAJAX × Scoobert Doobert; now Sanguine Penguin · ISRC QZES52289434 · OFF POP); do what i wanna (Mar 30, 2022 · originally Kajax, re-released as Sanguine Penguin · ISRC QZMEN2214880); wowowow (Apr 9, 2023 · Sanguine Penguin × Scoobert Doobert · ISRC QZK6Q2330066).
