Sam Wilkes is connected to the Thornton School of Music, which Luke F. Walton graduated from. He was born July 22, 1991. He studied R&B and funk in the first Popular Music class, with Patrice Rushen and Leon "Ndugu" Chancler, alongside Luke and Rozzi Crane (Wikipedia; The New Yorker on Wilkes and Sam Gendel). In the summer of 2010, Luke and Crane booked their first tour together, up the California coast from San Diego to San Francisco. The Luke Walton Band played one bill. Crane's band was that same lineup with Wilkes on bass: Logan Shrewsbury on drums, Peter Lee Johnson on violin, Luke on guitar. Nick Campbell played bass on the LP and the showcase clips, and not on this road leg. After school, Wikipedia has him as Crane's bassist and musical director, on the 2015 Maroon 5 tour with her, then Scary Pockets and Knower (with Louis Cole and Sam Gendel), solo albums including Wilkes (2018) and Driving (2023), and the Music for Saxofone & Bass Guitar series on Leaving Records. This site has no studio release he and Luke put out together.
