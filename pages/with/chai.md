Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with CHAI. Status on site: complete. CHAI were a Nagoya-formed quartet built around twin frontwomen MANA and KANA with YUNA and YUUKI. Their core concept was NEO KAWAII: pop, punk, funk, hip-hop, dance, and bright visual language pushing against narrow Japanese beauty standards and turning “complexes” into art. Pitchfork's early Rising profile describes the whole project as an argument about self-acceptance and redefining cute. CHAI went from Japanese releases to PINK, PUNK, then WINK on Sub Pop, with U.S. and international press coverage. Sub Pop's artist page describes WINK as the album where remote collaboration became part of the band's sound and CHAI looked outward into an international community. Walton's first CHAI credit is from the WINK / WINK TOGETHER era. “Miracle (Scoobert Doobert Remix)” is on CHAI's 2022 WINK TOGETHER EP with remixes by Confidence Man, ZAZEN BOYS, Busy P, STUTS, and Beenzino, the official remix companion to their Sub Pop album cycle.
