Debut Pure Chaos co-produced with Sarah Tudzin (Illuminati Hotties); vocal arrangement on MUNA's Dancing On The Wall. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Lou Roy. Status on site: complete. Also credited as: Huxlee. Lou Roy is a singer-songwriter and producer in Los Angeles. She relaunched in late 2019 from the name Huxlee, her parents' middle names. She calls the sound anti-genre: an acoustic ballad into synth-pop, a cappella, alt-country, odd electronic pop, with Fiona Apple, Joni Mitchell, and HAIM in the writing. The debut EP is Your Friend, (2019). The album Pure Chaos (2022) was co-produced with Sarah Tudzin of Illuminati Hotties. Winter Versions (2022) is that album played acoustic. Singles include Uppercut, Valkyrie, and U.D.I.D. On Scoobert Doobert's I she sings background vocals on six tracks: the cycle, ez pz, watercolor sky, only the beginning, see you again?, and golden state of mind. That is the album's "everything sings" stack, with Nicole McCabe on bass clarinet and Bubby Lewis on bass. Before that she is the feature vocal on angeleyes, the ABBA cover out August 12, 2022 (ISRC QZMEP2221941).
