LA “future indie” duo (Jason + Rand). Two Scoobert Doobert joint releases (feature vocals, not production credits): 3:00 (2023) and Hello (2024, second single from debut album Monterrey). Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Limón Limón. Status on site: complete. Limón Limón is a Southern California bedroom-pop / future-indie duo: Jason + Rand: with a feelgood, laidback catalog on Bandcamp. Monterrey is their 2024 debut album title; the duo is LA-based. Luke Francis Walton appears on the Scoobert side of both collabs: feature / co-release vocals, not mixing or production for Limón Limón. 3:00 (2023) landed first; York Calling on airy, Moon Safari–leaning indie pop. Hello followed as the second Monterrey single (2024), Melodic Mag on Latin-leaning R&B/pop; B-Side Guys frames it as a bittersweet farewell toast. Both tracks sit on the Monterrey tracklist; a third-party Alpas, Helius & Sarco remix of 3:00 followed in 2024. Collaborations: Hello (2024 · Monterrey · Melodic Mag, B-Side Guys); 3:00 (2023 · also on Monterrey · York Calling); 3:00: Alpas, Helius & Sarco Remix (2024 · third-party remix). Press on Limón Limón releases: York Calling: 3:00, Melodic Mag: Hello, B-Side Guys: Hello, Aipate: Monterrey / Origami, Limón Limón: Bandcamp.
