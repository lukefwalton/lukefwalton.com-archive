### With Bubby Lewis

**Summary.** Session and touring bassist. Bass on Scoobert Doobert’s Tired of the Sunshine (Moonlight Beach, 2023), singing for u, it gets better, and golden state of mind on I (2024), chasing the sunset (2024), and LOL and THIS IS FINE (2025). Love Music More guest, September 12, 2023.

Session and touring bassist. Love Music More guest, September 12, 2023. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Bubby Lewis. Status on site: complete. Bubby Lewis plays bass on Scoobert Doobert records. Tired of the Sunshine on Moonlight Beach (2023) bills him as the featured player.
