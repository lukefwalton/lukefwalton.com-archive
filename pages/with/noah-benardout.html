Singer, guitarist, and songwriter. Fronted Exist Elsewhere with Luke Walton, Brandon Woodward, and Nick Petrou (413, 2013); Luke and Brandon played in his band for his July 2012 Hard Rock Cafe Hollywood residency. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Noah Benardout. Status on site: partial. Noah Benardout sang and played guitar and keys in Exist Elsewhere. The band began as his backing band: in July 2012 he played a Friday residency at Hard Rock Cafe Hollywood, billed under his own name, with Luke Walton and Brandon Woodward in the band. GigTown dates Exist Elsewhere's birth to the last night of that residency, July 27, 2012. With Nick Petrou on bass (after Aaron Dowler), the band released 413 on August 2, 2013, including Tokyo, whose Kinect-shot video Andrew Gant directed. Luke and Brandon left to start FEiN; Noah kept Exist Elsewhere going with a new lineup into 2014. He later recorded as LOVR®. Noah was killed by a drunk driver on August 1, 2019. The Noah Benardout Foundation advocates DUI prevention in his memory. Please consider a donation to the foundation.
