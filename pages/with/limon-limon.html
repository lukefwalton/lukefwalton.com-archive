LA “future indie” duo (Jason + Rand). Two Scoobert Doobert joint releases (feature vocals, not production credits): 3:00 (2023) and Hello (2024, second single from debut album Monterrey). Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Limón Limón. Status on site: complete. Limón Limón is Jason and Rand, a bedroom-pop duo in Los Angeles, with a laid-back catalog on Bandcamp. Monterrey is the 2024 debut album. On both songs with them, Luke sings. He did not mix or produce the Limón Limón records. 3:00 came first, in 2023. York Calling heard airy indie pop with a Moon Safari lean. Hello was the second single from Monterrey, in 2024. Melodic Mag heard Latin-leaning R&B and pop. B-Side Guys heard a farewell toast. Both songs are on Monterrey. A third-party remix of 3:00 by Alpas, Helius and Sarco followed in 2024. Collaborations: Hello (2024 · Monterrey · Melodic Mag, B-Side Guys); 3:00 (2023 · also on Monterrey · York Calling); 3:00: Alpas, Helius & Sarco Remix (2024 · third-party remix).
