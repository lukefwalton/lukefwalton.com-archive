With KOMAGOME

Summary. KOMAGOME are YUUKA and CHIHIRO (ex-つぼみ大革命), produced by MANA and KANA after CHAI disbanded. Walton produced WE CAN'T DOLL (Aug 2025) and co-produced, mixed, and performed on CHAHHAN (Feb 2026). Uta-Net indexes 作曲 Scoobert Doobert・MANA・KANA on both singles.

KOMAGOME are YUUKA and CHIHIRO (ex-つぼみ大革命), produced by MANA and KANA after CHAI disbanded. Walton produced WE CAN'T DOLL (Aug 2025) and co-produced, mixed, and performed on CHAHHAN (Feb 2026). Uta-Net indexes 作曲 Scoobert Doobert・MANA・KANA on both singles. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with KOMAGOME. Status on site: complete. Rolling Stone Japan and ORICON frame WE CAN'T DOLL as CHAI解散後初プロデュース: MANA/KANA's first production credit after the band ended. Uta-Net also lists the single as 週刊ナイナイミュージック ending theme. KOMAGOME asked the twins to help aim for a new KAWAII; B-PASS describes the result as underground pop (punk, funk, pop fused), distinct from their idol past. Walton's credits are public on both singles: Apple Music credits WE CAN'T DOLL with Scoobert Doobert as arranger and composer alongside MANA/KANA; Uta-Net indexes CHAHHAN with the same lyric/composition split. Produced on the first; co-produced, mixed, and performed on the follow-up. Walton also mixed OKAME's DEBUT. Unit: つぼみ大革命 (Tsubomi Daikakumei) (YUUKA & CHIHIRO, ex-members). Production & composition: CHAHHAN (Feb 2026 · 作詞 MANA & KANA · 作曲 Scoobert Doobert, MANA & KANA · co-produced · mixed · performed); WE CAN'T DOLL (Aug 2025 · 作詞 MANA & KANA · 作曲 Scoobert Doobert, MANA & KANA · produced · official MV). Press on KOMAGOME releases: Rolling Stone Japan: post-CHAI first produce, ORICON: WE CAN'T DOLL / 解散後初プロデュース, B-PASS: underground pop / new KAWAII, Yoshimoto: WE CAN'T DOLL single, PR TIMES: CHAHHAN / oneman, Jungle Life: CHAHHAN credits, Uta-Net: composer index (Scoobert・MANA・KANA), Uta-Net: KOMAGOME, UtaTen: Scoobert Doobert, Uta-Net: WE CAN'T DOLL, Uta-Net: CHAHHAN, つぼみ大革命: Wikipedia, KOMAGOME: official site, WE CAN'T DOLL (official MV).
