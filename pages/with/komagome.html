YUUKA and CHIHIRO, formerly of つぼみ大革命, with MANA and KANA producing after CHAI. Luke produced WE CAN'T DOLL in August 2025 and co-produced, mixed, and played on CHAHHAN in February 2026. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with KOMAGOME. Status on site: complete. Rolling Stone Japan and ORICON describe WE CAN'T DOLL as MANA and KANA's first production after CHAI ended. Uta-Net also lists the single as the ending theme of 週刊ナイナイミュージック. KOMAGOME had asked the twins for help toward a new kind of kawaii. B-PASS hears punk and funk in it, and not the idol music YUUKA and CHIHIRO came from. The credits are public. On WE CAN'T DOLL, Apple Music lists Scoobert Doobert as arranger and composer beside MANA and KANA. Uta-Net gives CHAHHAN the same split: words by MANA and KANA, music by Scoobert Doobert with them. He produced the first single. On the second he co-produced, mixed, and played. He also mixed OKAME's album DEBUT. Unit: つぼみ大革命 (Tsubomi Daikakumei) (YUUKA & CHIHIRO, ex-members).
