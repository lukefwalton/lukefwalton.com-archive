### With Patrice Rushen

**Summary.** Pianist, composer, and music director; chair of USC Thornton's Popular Music Program 2013–2023. Luke studied music direction with Patrice Rushen at USC Thornton. Teacher and mentor.

Pianist, composer, and music director; chair of USC Thornton's Popular Music Program 2013–2023. Luke studied music direction with Patrice Rushen at USC Thornton. Teacher and mentor. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Patrice Rushen. Status on site: partial. Teacher and mentor. Luke studied music direction with Rushen; no shared recording credit. Luke F. Walton is the USC musician; NBA coach Luke Walton (Luke Theodore Walton) attended Arizona, not USC. Patrice Rushen is a Grammy-nominated pianist, composer, arranger, and music director. Her 1982 "Forget Me Nots" became the bassline for Will Smith's "Men in Black" (1997). She was the first woman to serve as musical director for the Grammy Awards, the Emmy Awards, and the NAACP Image Awards. From 2013 to 2023 she chaired USC Thornton's Popular Music Program (USC Thornton, NEA Jazz Masters). Luke studied music direction with Patrice Rushen at USC Thornton.
