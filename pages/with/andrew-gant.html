Director. Directed Exist Elsewhere's Kinect-shot Tokyo music video (2013) for Private School Entertainment. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Andrew Gant. Status on site: partial. Andrew Gant directed the video for Tokyo, from Exist Elsewhere's 413, for Private School Entertainment in 2013, shooting the band with a Kinect, a DSLR, and a projector through RGBDToolkit. No Film School interviewed him about the rig in August 2013; he credits Sarah Oh with the lightman art. His portfolio says the project pushed his work toward narrative film and volumetric capture.
