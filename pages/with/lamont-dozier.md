# With Lamont Dozier

## Record

### With Lamont Dozier

**Summary.** Legendary songwriter and producer (Holland–Dozier–Holland / Motown). At USC Thornton, Luke studied songwriting with Lamont Dozier. Teacher and mentor.

Legendary songwriter and producer (Holland–Dozier–Holland / Motown). At USC Thornton, Luke studied songwriting with Lamont Dozier. Teacher and mentor. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Lamont Dozier. Status on site: partial. Teacher and mentor at USC Thornton. There is no shared songwriting credit on a public release. Lamont Dozier is one of the architects of the Motown sound: co-author (with Brian and Eddie Holland) of "Stop! In the Name of Love," "Reach Out I'll Be There," "How Sweet It Is (To Be Loved by You)," and dozens more. Holland–Dozier–Holland defined the classic Motown era. Dozier later taught songwriting at the USC Thornton School of Music. Luke studied songwriting with Lamont Dozier at USC. The DOLO Los Angeles showcase archive lists Luke Walton among USC Thornton Popular Music students from the same program cohort. Dozier was his teacher and mentor; there is no shared songwriting credit on a public release. Teacher / mentor: USC Thornton: Songwriting with Lamont Dozier (Studied songwriting with Lamont Dozier at USC). Press on Lamont Dozier releases: Wikipedia: Lamont Dozier, DOLO Los Angeles: Luke Walton / USC Thornton.
