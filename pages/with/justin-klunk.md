### With Justin Klunk

**Summary.** LA saxophonist and session player. Luke Francis Walton played guitar on all five tracks of Justin Klunk – EP (Jul 19, 2013). Klunk later played baritone, alto, and tenor sax on FEiN's Little Homes (2016).

LA saxophonist and session player. Luke Francis Walton played guitar on all five tracks of Justin Klunk – EP (Jul 19, 2013). Klunk later played baritone, alto, and tenor sax on FEiN's Little Homes (2016). Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Justin Klunk. Status on site: complete. The 2013 EP credits (Justin Klunk – EP): Justin Klunk saxophone · Gabe Rudner keyboard · Nick Campbell bass · Luke Walton guitar · Nate Laguzza drums, five songs, 22 minutes, self-released July 19, 2013 (Spotify · Apple Music). Klunk's second solo EP Clarity (2016, produced by Klunk & Matias Mora (Ain't It Fun), Devon Jane guitar, Gabe Rudner keys, Kurtis Keber / Nick Campbell bass, Nate Laguzza drums) is on the same justinklunk.com/media discography page; Luke is on the 2013 debut EP, not Clarity. Klunk returned on the FEiN album Little Homes (May 31, 2016): baritone sax on the instrumental Intro and multi-sax on Girl You Can't Hide It, documented by a Facebook session video (audio) of the baritone tracking pass. He later worked in broader LA session and live contexts (including Saint Motel and related pop/sax credits). With Annie Dingwall he later formed alt-pop duo RAINNE; archive live photo documents Luke on guitar with Dingwall and Klunk at an Annie Dingwall concert.
