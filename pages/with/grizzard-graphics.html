With Grizzard Graphics

Summary. Illustrator and designer behind the core Scoobert Doobert visual identity: the SD monogram, the LOVE y? mark, and the Love Music More logo, and the album covers from Dragon Ball $d (2020) through I (2024). Credited as Grizzard Graphics (grizzardgfx). Luke Brogoitti.

Illustrator and designer behind the core Scoobert Doobert visual identity: the SD monogram, the LOVE y? mark, and the Love Music More logo, and the album covers from Dragon Ball $d (2020) through I (2024). Credited as Grizzard Graphics (grizzardgfx). Luke Brogoitti. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Grizzard Graphics. Status on site: complete. Also credited as: grizzardgfx, Luke Brogoitti. Grizzard Graphics is Luke Brogoitti. He illustrated and designed the Scoobert Doobert covers from Dragon Ball $d (2020) through I (2024): Masks and Monsters, Little Hug, Big Hug, KŌAN, Moonlight Beach, and MÖB, and the single art in those cycles. His portfolio is the account of that run. Illustration and design on I are also credited on the vinyl insert and Bandcamp booklet. He also drew last first date (2024), the US chapter singles (2025): Main Character Energy, Party, After-Party, Jazz Flute, and LOL, and the standalone covers You Make Me Feel Like Dancing, when it's over (Sugar Ray), Souvenir (Bump of Chicken), PLACEBO + 野田洋次郎, Michael Bublé, My Santa Buddy, Winter Wonderland, and graphic design on wannagetaway (2026). Beyond covers, Grizzard Graphics drew the marks in the press kit: the Scoobert SD monogram, the LOVE y? graphic, and the Love Music More stacked wordmark. Portfolio, links, and socials are under Elsewhere below.
