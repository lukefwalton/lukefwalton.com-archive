With Lara Johnston

Lara Johnston is a vocalist, songwriter, and live performer whose father is Tom Johnston, co-founder of The Doobie Brothers. Lara's band opened on Doobie Brothers tour dates. Luke played electric and acoustic guitar in Lara's band, the same role on every date. The dates run roughly 2012–2015, between Luke's USC years and the FEiN records. With Lara's band, Luke played Doobie Brothers dates (Lara's band joined the Doobies on stage for "Listen to the Music" and sometimes other songs), Gregg Allman support tours, and festival and venue dates across North America and Canada. Setlist.fm records confirm several dates. Luke's public Instagram serves as a first-person archive of the period. In the studio: Out of My Mind (FEiN x Lara Johnston) went up on SoundCloud on June 28, 2015, the first release in the FEiN Times series. FEiN produced and recorded it at Tiny Giant, and Frank Rosato mixed and mastered it. Fresh Fruit (FEiN x Lara Johnston) followed on September 15, 2015. That SoundCloud credit block names Luke Walton (guitar/keys), Brandon Woodward (drums/percussion/keys), Lara Johnston (vocals), and production and recording by FEiN at Tiny Giant. Both songs are co-written by Walton, Woodward and Johnston, a third each (Luke Walton's account).
