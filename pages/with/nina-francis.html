Walton graduated from. ninafrancismusic.com leads with the 2020 San Diego Music Awards Best Singer-Songwriter. She grew up in San Diego and studied Popular Music there (Bishop's School 2010; mentors include Randy Newman and Lamont Dozier, per the San Diego Reader). The April 2014 senior Pop Showcase at the Troubadour put FEiN, Nina Francis, and Nick Belcher on the same bill, before Boomerang (2016) and Between Dreams (2017). Between Dreams (Oct 7, 2017): eleven originals written by Nina Francis; produced by Luke Walton and Brandon Woodward at Tiny Giant Recording; mixed/mastered by Frank Rosato at Woodcliff Studios (bonus track recorded by Chris Sampson). San Diego Troubadour: “outstanding, must-have debut album”, arrangements from stark to full band around her voice. Boomerang (with FEiN) (February 2016) was written by Nina Francis and FEiN (Luke Walton, Brandon Woodward), and arranged, recorded, and produced by FEiN at Tiny Giant. 91X, the San Diego rock station, and 94.9 played it. Walton co-wrote Boomerang. He did not write Between Dreams. Production: Between Dreams (Oct 2017 LP · produced (with Woodward) · Francis wrote all originals).
