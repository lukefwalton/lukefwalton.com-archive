Bassist. Exist Elsewhere's bassist on 413 (2013) with Noah Benardout, Luke Walton, and Brandon Woodward; bass on two Rees Finley songs Luke and Brandon recorded at Tiny Giant (2017–2018). Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Nick Petrou. Status on site: partial. Nick Petrou played bass and sang in Exist Elsewhere after Aaron Dowler, with Noah Benardout, Luke Walton, and Brandon Woodward. That lineup played the Macworld Sonicbids presentation (January 31, 2013) and released 413 on August 2, 2013; a Getty photo from the Sunset Strip Music Festival the same day captions him as the bassist. Around 2013 he is in a photo with Luke and Justin Klunk at a Barry Harris show. He played bass on Rees Finley's I Don't Want To Walk Away (2017) and Hate To See You (2018), which Brandon Woodward and Luke recorded at Tiny Giant.
