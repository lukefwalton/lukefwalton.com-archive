# With Jamie Drake

## Record

### With Jamie Drake

**Summary.** LA singer-songwriter (debut Everything's Fine: NPR, PopMatters, Flood, Atwood). Featured vocalist on Moonlight Beach's Meteor Shower, which Luke Francis Walton wrote; Scoobert Doobert remix of her 2023 single New Girl.

LA singer-songwriter (debut Everything's Fine: NPR, PopMatters, Flood, Atwood). Featured vocalist on Moonlight Beach's Meteor Shower, which Luke Francis Walton wrote; Scoobert Doobert remix of her 2023 single New Girl. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Jamie Drake. Status on site: complete. Jamie Drake is an LA-based singer-songwriter whose debut Everything's Fine drew national press: NPR on Redwood Tree, PopMatters on the debut's charm, Flood on Oh Well Oh Well, Atwood and AudioFemme on the LP's pop-and-cinematic blend. Bandcamp carries the full discography including New Girl (the remix source track). Two Scoobert Doobert credits: (1) featured vocal on Meteor Shower, track seven on Moonlight Beach. Luke Francis Walton wrote it. Drake sings on it; (2) remix client on New Girl (Scoobert Doobert Remix) (Apr 14, 2023, Antifragile Music). @jamiethedrake announced the remix Apr 10, 2023 (Molly Tuttle Design art). On tour after release, Drake closed every set with a dance party to the Scoobert remix, per Luke Walton's own account. Collaborations: Meteor Shower (2023 · Moonlight Beach · featured vocal · Luke Francis Walton, writer · ISRC QZES52386550); Moonlight Beach (LP context · Drake on track 7). Remix: New Girl (Scoobert Doobert Remix) (2023 · Antifragile Music · Luke Francis Walton remix · tour set closer dance party). Press on Jamie Drake releases: Jamie Drake: New Girl remix announce, Jamie Drake (official), Bandcamp: Thread and the Spark / New Girl.
