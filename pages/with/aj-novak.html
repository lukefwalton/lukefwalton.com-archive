With A.J. Novak

Summary. Drummer. USC Thornton: Checkpoint Six (2011) with Luke Walton and Rob Nagelhout.

Drummer. USC Thornton: Checkpoint Six (2011) with Luke Walton and Rob Nagelhout. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with A.J. Novak. Status on site: partial. USC-era work. Checkpoint Six recorded four songs that were never released. A.J. Novak played drums in Checkpoint Six, the 2011 USC Thornton false start Luke co-created: Walton on vocals and guitar, Rob Nagelhout on bass, Novak on drums. Walton wrote the four songs and the three recorded them; one show, nothing released. The Daily Trojan's January 2011 interview with the band MEGG names Novak (drums) and Nagelhout as Thornton sophomores. With Luke Walton: Checkpoint Six (2011) (Walton vocals, guitar · Rob Nagelhout bass · Novak drums · four songs, one show · unreleased). Press on A.J. Novak releases: A.J. Novak: official site, Daily Trojan: Interview with MEGG (Jan 10, 2011). Related hubs: Rob Nagelhout.
