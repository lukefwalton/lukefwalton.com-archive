# With Jordan Sorokin, Ph.D.

## Record

### With Jordan Sorokin, Ph.D.

**Summary.** Bass in Blue Suburbia (~2008–2009), which he co-founded as an equal with Luke Walton and Zach Freeman. Advisory board, Surmado, Inc. Researcher; Stanford-era Bay Area conversations on AI with Luke F. Walton (circa 2014–2019).

Jordan Sorokin, Ph.D. co-founded Blue Suburbia, the La Costa Canyon blues trio, as an equal with Luke Walton and Zach Freeman, and played bass on its 2009 EP The Other Side. He later served on the advisory board of Surmado, Inc. (Luke F. Walton, founder), alongside Jonathan Gillie. After Blue Suburbia he went to the University of Pennsylvania. While at Stanford (circa 2014–2019), Walton and Sorokin walked the redwood forests of the Bay Area talking about AI, before Surmado's product work and Walton's authorship and answerability research.
