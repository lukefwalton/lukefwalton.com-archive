With Annie Dingwall

Summary. USC Thornton Popular Music singer-songwriter (BM ’13). Luke Walton played guitar in her live band with Justin Klunk (saxophone) at an Annie Dingwall concert. Dingwall later formed alt-pop duo RAINNE with Klunk.
