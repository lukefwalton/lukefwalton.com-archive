### With Great Good Fine Ok

**Summary.** Brooklyn-born synthpop duo (Jon Sandler + Luke Moellman) who relocated to Los Angeles in 2022. Love Music More guests; July 2022 Instagram documents a studio session with Luke Walton (Scoobert Doobert). The Beformer OFF POP playlist post listed Bumblebae next to GGFO.

Great Good Fine Ok (GGFO) is an American synthpop band formed in Brooklyn in 2013 by Jon Sandler and Luke Moellman. Wikipedia documents Hype Machine #1 runs, Neon Gold / Epic signing, Chainsmokers feature Let You Go, and a 2022 LA relocation, the same year @greatgoodfineok posted a studio photo with Scoobert Doobert (July 28, 2022; screenshot kept on file). On this site the relationship is a studio session and podcast episodes, not a released Scoobert × GGFO single: Jon Sandler and Luke Moellman on Love Music More; Danny Wolf (GGFO live drummer) on the same show. April 2022 Beformer OFF POP post lists Bumblebae next to GGFO on the playlist, adjacent editorial context, not a co-write credit.
