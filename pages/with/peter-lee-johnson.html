Los Angeles violinist, producer, and songwriter. Violin in The Luke Walton Band (USC Thornton · Goodbye/Hello, 2010); guitar on his 2012 Jason Mraz cover; violin on FEiN Little Homes (Creatures, Don't You). BMI songwriter on Lucky Daye's Roll Some Mo (2020 GRAMMY Best R&B Song nominee). Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Peter Lee Johnson. Status on site: complete. Also credited as: PLJ. Peter Lee Johnson met Luke in USC Thornton's first Popular Music Program, about 2009 to 2010. He played violin in The Luke Walton Band: Luke on guitar and voice, Logan Shrewsbury on drums, Nick Campbell on bass. Goodbye/Hello came out September 1, 2010. He was on the 2010 California coast tour bills with Rozzi Crane and Sam Wilkes. The same group is on the Winter 2009 USC Trojan Family Magazine cover, after Luke's Taylor Swift date video. On November 29, 2010, Walton uploaded Top 10 Acoustic Mashup: Peter Lee Johnson & Luke Walton to @lukefwalton: Billboard's top ten in one acoustic mashup, recorded live. Johnson played violin, Nick Campbell bass, Logan Shrewsbury drums, Walton guitar (Billboard Hot 100 top ten · every instrument recorded live · no samples from originals). There is a chord chart. On June 11, 2012, Johnson uploaded I Won't Give Up: Jason Mraz (violin cover) to @PeterLeeJohnson. Guitar and violin went down in one take, then piano, drums, shaker, and bass. The description credits Luke Walton on guitar (Thanks to my friend Luke Walton for layin' down the guitar on this one. (YouTube description · lukewaltonmusic.com)). After Thornton he built a YouTube and studio life at pljmusic.com: violin covers, production, and session work. BMI lists him among the 2020 GRAMMY nominees as co-writer of Lucky Daye's "Roll Some Mo" (Best R&B Song). A TEDxUSC bio from June 21, 2010 names him in The Luke Walton Band. On Little Homes he played violin on Creatures and Don't You. The credit is on the FEiN Times Issue 3 liner (the personnel block). That was session work. He did not co-write the FEiN songs.
