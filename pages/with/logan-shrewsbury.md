### With Logan Shrewsbury

**Summary.** Drummer. The Luke Walton Band (Goodbye/Hello, 2010), Rozzi Crane's 2010 coast-tour band, USC Thornton showcases and gigs through 2012, and Annie Dingwall's band (2014).

Drummer. The Luke Walton Band (Goodbye/Hello, 2010), Rozzi Crane's 2010 coast-tour band, USC Thornton showcases and gigs through 2012, and Annie Dingwall's band (2014). Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Logan Shrewsbury. Status on site: partial. Logan Shrewsbury played drums in The Luke Walton Band with Luke Walton, Peter Lee Johnson (violin), and Nick Campbell (bass), formed in USC Thornton's inaugural Popular Music Program in 2009–2010. He is credited on Goodbye/Hello and Dead End Love, and drummed in Rozzi Crane's band on the self-booked summer 2010 California coast tour: the same Walton lineup with Sam Wilkes on bass. He drummed on the 2010 Popular Music Showcase covers with Crane and Campbell, the November 29, 2010 Top 10 Acoustic Mashup, and the Del Mar Fair set on July 4, 2011, and performed at GRAMMY U Rock the Mic Night (April 9, 2012). He calls himself a founding member of Bear Attack, whose Shapes Luke helped engineer. In 2014 he drummed in Annie Dingwall's band with Luke.
