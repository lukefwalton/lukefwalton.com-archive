# With Casey McSocial

## Record

### With Casey McSocial

**Summary.** La Costa Canyon, 2006: Walton founded the Rock I IV V school club (named for the I–IV–V progression) and played bass in Casey McSocial, his first band, filling in while Sean Sobash went to college. Publicly traceable in the 2006–2007 San Diego all-ages scene via San Diego Reader listings: dated Jumping Turtle bills on October 25 and December 21, 2006 (inside his stated tenure), then February 18, 2007 at the Turtle and May 24 and July 27, 2007 at the Epicentre after it. The band's 2007 Spotify track "Natalie Brooks" postdates his tenure and is not a Luke Walton recording credit.

Casey McSocial was Walton's first band experience at La Costa Canyon (2006, bass). He then co-founded Mannequin (2006–2008, original songs, Jonathan Gillie on guitar). Blue Suburbia followed (2008–2009, classic rock / blues trio with Jordan Sorokin and Zach Freeman, EP The Other Side). All three ran in parallel with his self-released solo EPs Just A Friend (2006) and Heart in Hand (2008). Luke graduated early from La Costa Canyon in January 2009 and used the open semester on Six Months to Make It, a 21-episode vlog (Jan–July 2009) documenting the push from amateur to working musician before USC. Both bandmates are independently documented, just not as members of this band. Sean Sobash is a working touring and session bassist; his own Instagram bio lists tour credits with Luke Hemmings, Dominic Fike, Remi Wolf, Rooney, Franc Moody, Hether, Colin Blunstone, and Lulu Simon ("salty road dog specializing in bass"), alongside seansobash.com, IMDb, and The Career Musician podcast. His Shout Out LA interview (Aug 5, 2024) begins with a school band: "I started playing music with friends from school we had a garage band and participated in battle of the bands at school," then playing professionally in his late teens before he "moved two hours north to Los Angeles," which is North County San Diego geography. He names neither the school nor the band, and he does not date the college year, so this confirms the shape of the memory, not the lineup or the year. Todd McClintock is an animation writer in Carlsbad. His LinkedIn lists a BA in creative writing at UC Davis (August 2007–June 2011); production coordinator on season 1 of Amphibia (July 2017–December 2018) and staff writer for two seasons (January 2019–August 2021) at Disney Television Animation; staff writer on season 7 of The Amazing World of Gumball at Cartoon Network Studios (August 2022–October 2023); and staff writer at Nickelodeon Animation since November 2023. He also wrote the CoComelon Story Time podcast at Moonbug (August–November 2021). San Diego Writers, Ink calls him Emmy-nominated. The nomination is Outstanding Writing for an Animated Program for Amphibia at the 1st Children's and Family Emmy Awards (nominations announced November 1, 2022; ceremonies December 10–11, 2022). IMDb lists him as Todd Michael McClintock on that nomination. Maya and the Three won the category. None of that names Casey McSocial. The 2006 lineup remains his account.
