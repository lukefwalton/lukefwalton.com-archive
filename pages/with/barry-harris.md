### With Barry Harris

**Summary.** USC-era singer-songwriter (Barry Harris & His LA Band), not Barry Harris (1929–2023), the Detroit jazz pianist. Luke co-produced and co-arranged the original "Just a Mile Away" at USC Thornton. The 2023 streaming single re-recorded parts of it and does not list him.
