# With Embody

## Record

### With Embody

**Summary.** London-based Lithuanian producer Karolis Labanauskas, recording as Embody. FEiN featured on his Armada Deep single Remember Us (2016) and the 2021 re-edit.

London-based Lithuanian producer Karolis Labanauskas, recording as Embody. FEiN featured on his Armada Deep single Remember Us (2016) and the 2021 re-edit. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Embody. Status on site: partial. Also credited as: Karolis Labanauskas. Karolis Labanauskas is the producer behind the Embody artist credit on streaming and label releases. Luke Francis Walton and Brandon Woodward (FEiN) co-wrote the lyrics and melody on Remember Us (June 16, 2016, Armada Deep ARDP149); FEiN supplied the featured vocal. Remember Us (Re-Edit) (2021, Armada / The Bearded Man) carries the same Walton/Woodward writer credit. Deezer and Muso.AI list L. F. Walton, B. M. Woodward, and K. Labanauskas on the 2016 release; L. F. Walton is Luke Francis Walton (BMI songwriter IPI). The 2021 Re-Edit Spotify credits pane lists Luke Francis Walton and Brandon Michael Woodward as Composer · Lyricist and Karolis Labanauskas as Composer · Producer. FEiN topline on Embody production, not a Scoobert or Luke Walton production credit. Don Diablo: Hexagon Radio Episode 045 (Dec 9, 2015 SoundCloud upload) opens with Embody – Remember Us (feat. FEiN) as track 1. Episode description: broadcast in 35+ countries; syndication on Evolution / iHeart Radio (USA), Fun Radio (France), and SLAM FM (Netherlands), radio play ahead of the Jun 16, 2016 Armada Deep single. Collaborations with FEiN: Remember Us (Extended Mix) (Jun 16, 2016 · Embody feat. FEiN · Armada Deep ARDP149 · ISRC NLF711603322); Hexagon Radio Episode 045 (Don Diablo) (Dec 2015 upload · Remember Us track 1 · 35+ countries · iHeart / Fun Radio / SLAM FM); Remember Us (Re-Edit) (Apr 23, 2021 · Armada / The Bearded Man · Spotify credits: Luke Francis Walton & Brandon Michael Woodward (Composer · Lyricist)). Press on Embody releases: Discogs: Embody Feat. FEiN: Remember Us, Remember Us (official video), Muso.AI: Luke Francis Walton profile (Remember Us · L. F. Walton), Spotify credits: Remember Us (Re-Edit), Don Diablo: Hexagon Radio Episode 045. Related hubs: FEiN hub (Luke Walton + Brandon Woodward).
