# With Grizzard Graphics

### With Grizzard Graphics

**Summary.** Illustrator and designer behind the core Scoobert Doobert visual identity: the SD monogram, the LOVE y? mark, and the Love Music More logo, and the album covers from Dragon Ball $d (2020) through I (2024). Credited as Grizzard Graphics (grizzardgfx). Luke Brogoitti.

Illustrator and designer behind the core Scoobert Doobert visual identity: the SD monogram, the LOVE y? mark, and the Love Music More logo, and the album covers from Dragon Ball $d (2020) through I (2024). Credited as Grizzard Graphics (grizzardgfx). Luke Brogoitti. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Grizzard Graphics. Status on site: complete. Also credited as: Luke Brogoitti, grizzardgfx. Grizzard Graphics is Luke Brogoitti. He illustrated and designed the Scoobert Doobert covers from Dragon Ball $d (2020) through I (2024): Masks and Monsters, Little Hug, Big Hug, KŌAN, Moonlight Beach, and MÖB, and the single art in those cycles. His portfolio is the account of that run. Illustration and design on I are also credited on the vinyl insert and Bandcamp booklet. He also drew last first date (2024), the US chapter singles (2025): Main Character Energy, Party, After-Party, Jazz Flute, and LOL, and the standalone covers You Make Me Feel Like Dancing, when it's over (Sugar Ray), Souvenir (Bump of Chicken), PLACEBO + 野田洋次郎, Michael Bublé, My Santa Buddy, Winter Wonderland, and graphic design on wannagetaway (2026). Beyond covers, Grizzard Graphics drew the marks in the brand kit: the Scoobert SD monogram, the LOVE y? graphic, and the Love Music More stacked wordmark. Portfolio, links, and socials are under Elsewhere below. He works at NAMM. He photographed Luke Walton with Derek Ali at the TEC Awards step-and-repeat (that frame). Album & cover art: Dragon Ball $d (2020 · first Scoobert cover by Grizzard Graphics); Masks and Monsters (2020); Little Hug (2021); Big Hug (2021); KŌAN (2022); Moonlight Beach (2023); MÖB (2023); I (2024 · Beformer · vinyl insert / Bandcamp booklet); 5G BABY (2021 · Scoobert Doobert × Robel Ketema); Alright (2025); US (2025 · chapter single covers); You Make Me Feel Like Dancing (2025 · Leo Sayer cover); when it's over (2025 · Sugar Ray cover); Souvenir (2023 · Bump of Chicken cover); PLACEBO + 野田洋次郎 (2022 · Kenshi Yonezu cover); Michael Bublé, My Santa Buddy (2023 · sweater single); Winter Wonderland (2023 · sweater single); last first date (2024 · single cover); wannagetaway (2026 · DistroKid graphic design).
