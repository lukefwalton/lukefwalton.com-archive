With Nick Campbell

Summary. Los Angeles bassist, composer, and educator. Bass on The Luke Walton Band’s Goodbye/Hello (2010); Thornton-era showcases with Walton and Rozzi Crane; Justin Klunk EP (2013). Records as Nick Campbell Destroys; session work with Pomplamoose, Scary Pockets, Vulfpeck, Charlie Puth, and Meghan Trainor; Scott’s Bass Lessons tutor.

Los Angeles bassist, composer, and educator. Bass on The Luke Walton Band’s Goodbye/Hello (2010); Thornton-era showcases with Walton and Rozzi Crane; Justin Klunk EP (2013). Records as Nick Campbell Destroys; session work with Pomplamoose, Scary Pockets, Vulfpeck, Charlie Puth, and Meghan Trainor; Scott’s Bass Lessons tutor. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Nick Campbell. Status on site: complete. Also credited as: Nick "Thor" Campbell. Nick Campbell was the bassist in The Luke Walton Band on Goodbye/Hello (September 1, 2010): Logan Shrewsbury drums, Peter Lee Johnson violin, Campbell bass, Luke Walton guitar and voice. Walton called him “Thor” in college, a nickname that stuck on the LP credits and in later interviews (Growing as an Artist). The Bear Attack Bandcamp about line also names Campbell and Shrewsbury, with Eric Radloff, Mia Minichiello, and Brandon Bae. The same Thornton cohort ran weekly LA shows. On the 2010 California coast tour (San Diego → San Francisco, Walton and Rozzi Crane’s first tour together, self-booked), The Luke Walton Band ran one bill while Crane’s band was the same Walton lineup with Sam Wilkes on bass (Nick Campbell played bass on the LP and showcase clips, not that road leg). Surviving Popular Music Showcase 2010 footage documents Crane vocals with Walton guitar and Campbell bass on Toto and Jill Scott covers. Campbell returned on Justin Klunk – EP (July 19, 2013): Klunk sax · Gabe Rudner keys · Campbell bass · Walton guitar · Nate Laguzza drums. Campbell is an LA session bassist and composer. SoundBetter and La Bella Strings list sideman work with Pomplamoose, Scary Pockets, Vulfpeck, Charlie Puth, Meghan Trainor, Nigel Hall, Moonchild, and others. Under Nick Campbell Destroys he releases industrial jazz-funk originals: albums Art (Dec 2022) and Live for the Highest Bidder (Jul 2023), and teaches on Scott’s Bass Lessons. The Luke Walton Band: Goodbye/Hello (Sep 2010 LP · Campbell bass · Walton guitar · Shrewsbury drums · Peter Lee Johnson violin); Rosanna (Toto cover): Popular Music Showcase 2010 (Campbell bass · Logan Shrewsbury drums · Walton guitar · Rozzi Crane vocals · /music/#selected-videos); A Long Walk (Jill Scott cover) (Campbell bass · Logan Shrewsbury drums · Walton guitar · Crane vocals · Thornton-era showcase archive). Other sessions with Luke Walton: Justin Klunk – EP recording session (~2013) (Released July 19, 2013 · Campbell bass · posted February 13, 2014); Justin Klunk – EP (all five tracks) (Jul 19, 2013 · Campbell bass · Walton guitar). Press on Nick Campbell releases: Nick Campbell Destroys: Art (Bandcamp), Nick Campbell Destroys: Live for the Highest Bidder (Bandcamp), SoundBetter: Nick Campbell Destroys, Scott's Bass Lessons: Nick Campbell (tutor), La Bella Strings: Nick Campbell, Love Music More: Growing as an Artist (Walton on “Thor”). Related hubs: Goodbye/Hello (Luke Walton Band LP), With Rozzi Crane (2010 showcase & coast tour), With Sam Wilkes (2010 coast tour bass), With Justin Klunk (2013 EP), With Peter Lee Johnson (Luke Walton Band violin), With Bear Attack (Bandcamp about line).
