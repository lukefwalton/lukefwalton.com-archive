# With TAKATSU-KING

## Record

### With TAKATSU-KING

**Summary.** Shingo Murakami's long-running rapper persona, the billed artist on だれかおるやろ feat. CHAI, the theme for Murakami's own program 『村上信五のだれおる！』 (Lemino, April 19, 2023). Luke Francis Walton produced, mixed, co-engineered and co-arranged the theme and played drums, bass and guitar; MANA and KANA of CHAI wrote it.

Shingo Murakami's long-running rapper persona, the billed artist on だれかおるやろ feat. CHAI, the theme for Murakami's own program 『村上信五のだれおる！』 (Lemino, April 19, 2023). Luke Francis Walton produced, mixed, co-engineered and co-arranged the theme and played drums, bass and guitar; MANA and KANA of CHAI wrote it. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with TAKATSU-KING. Status on site: partial. Also credited as: TAKATSU-KING (Shingo Murakami), Shingo Murakami, 村上信五. The persona predates this theme: Toshinobu Kubota wrote and produced the earlier TAKATSU-KING single 王様クリニック (2016), and it had already been on Kanjani Eight stages and in commercials. This hub is the act as billed; Murakami's own name appears on the song page. だれかおるやろ feat. CHAI is the theme for Murakami's program, performed by his TAKATSU-KING persona featuring CHAI, with lyrics and music by MANA and KANA. Walton produced, mixed, co-engineered and co-arranged it and played drums, bass and guitar. The show relaunched on Lemino on April 19, 2023 with a 49-minute premiere about the song. The theme is registered with JASRAC (284-9813-5) and was never released on CD or streaming. Production: だれかおるやろ feat. CHAI (Lemino theme · Apr 19, 2023 premiere · produced, mixed, co-engineered, co-arranged; drums, bass, guitar · lyrics and music MANA, KANA).
