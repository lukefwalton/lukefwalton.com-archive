### With Joshoo

**Summary.** Trumpeter, horn arranger, and multi-instrumentalist (Josh Shpak / Joshoo). Trumpeter, horn arranger, and multi-instrumentalist (Josh Shpak / Joshoo). Josh Shpak records and tours as Joshoo, Berklee-trained trumpeter, horn arranger, and producer (Clark Terry protégé; Yamaha artist page). Before the Gabriel work he was the trumpet player in Ripe, the Berklee-originated Boston/LA funk/alt-pop band: Josh Shpak and Calvin Barthel on horns (trumpet and trombone) from the 2011 founding lineup per Wikipedia) and Discogs. The Peter Gabriel credits are on public record. Shpak joined i/o The Tour (2023) on trumpet, French horn, EWI, keyboards, and vocals: Gabriel's engineer Oli Jacobs overheard him rehearsing next door and recommended him (At The Barrier, PeterGabriel.com). Album credits: trumpet on Road to Joy and Olive Tree (Pitchfork, Wikipedia I/O)). On Olive Tree, session detail includes home-studio trumpet overdubs (summer 2022) and a stacked horn arrangement for a full-section sound. With Scoobert Doobert: Ok Ok OK (2023, Joshoo × Scoobert Doobert), co-written by Josh Shpak and Luke Francis Walton per streaming metadata. Same person discussed touring, sync, and Apple commercials on Love Music More. Co-writing: Ok Ok OK (2023 · Joshoo × Scoobert Doobert · Shpak + Walton writers).
