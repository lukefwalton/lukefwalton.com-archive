Sam Friend founded Beformer (Beformer LLC, also known as Beformer Records), an independent record label and artist-services company, also on Instagram and Facebook. Scoobert Doobert is Luke F. Walton's primary music project. Scoobert Doobert is signed to Beformer, and some releases are Beformer × indiemono co-releases. indiemono is a playlist and distribution partner. Luke founded Scoobert Doobert and owns it and its masters. He did not found Beformer, does not own it, and does not work for it. Beformer and Luke co-own Love Music More, the newsletter and podcast Luke hosts, and Beformer produces it. Sam handles the show's sponsorship inquiries. Beformer also published Luke's audiobook of Plato's Apology (2023) and released the Burrito Bot albums. Beformer's own posts are part of the record: the Spotify editorial playlist adds collected in the catalog, and the August 25, 2022 post about Can't Imagine Feeling Better in a Coors Light Canada TV campaign. Releases on Beformer: Big Hug (2021 · Scoobert Doobert · Mundane Magazine: released on Beformer Records); Protoplasmic Prison Cell (2022 · I Don't Speak French × Scoobert Doobert · Beformer / Indieshake); I (2024 · Scoobert Doobert · released on Beformer); Refried Being (2024 · Burrito Bot · on Beformer); Wrap Music (2024 · Burrito Bot · on Beformer); in it with u (2024 · Scoobert Doobert × India Thieriot · on Beformer).
