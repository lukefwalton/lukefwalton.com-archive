Swedish eclectic alt-rock/pop project (often stylized IDSF), founded and produced by multi-instrumentalist Håkan Persson after university. Rotating collaborators; psychedelic, punk, and indie pop with sync placements (MTV, Samsung). Two Scoobert Doobert joint singles on Indieshake. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with I Don't Speak French. Status on site: complete. Also credited as: IDSF. I Don't Speak French is a Swedish project led by Håkan Persson, who plays, produces, and runs the Indieshake label. The band started after music-production school. The name is an early lyric: "I don't speak French, or Italian baby." The style moves on purpose, from samba to punk to a big hook. The catalog includes The Archives, Pt. 1 and Pt. 2 (2022), and Blow the Speakers Up on the album BOB. The songs have been on MTV, including Catfish and Teen Mom, and in Samsung campaigns outside Sweden. On the two singles with Scoobert Doobert, Luke is the named collaborator. He did not mix Persson's records. The singles are Protoplasmic Prison Cell, September 30, 2022, on Beformer and Indieshake, and Voulez-Vous, August 4, 2023, on Indieshake. On August 9, 2023, @indieshake and @beformer noted Voulez-Vous on Spotify's New Music Friday, Indie Covers, and Apple Music's New Music Daily. Persson talked about DIY, Sweden, and sync on Love Music More. Collaborations with Scoobert Doobert: Protoplasmic Prison Cell (2022 · Beformer / Indieshake · ISRC QZNWU2290488); Voulez-Vous (2023 · Indieshake · NMF / Indie Covers / Apple NMD · ISRC QMBZ92313382). Love Music More: DIY, Sweden, and Sync with Håkan Persson (IDSF founder · Swedish Red Elephant · Indie Shake). Press on I Don't Speak French releases: Indieshake × Beformer: Voulez-Vous editorial carousel, Beyond The Stage: Blow the Speakers Up / Samsung, B-Sides: Indieshake / sync interview, Jungle Indie Rock: Can You See premiere.
