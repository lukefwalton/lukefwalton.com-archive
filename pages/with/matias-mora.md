# With Matias Mora

## Record

### With Matias Mora

**Summary.** Writer, producer, and engineer in Los Angeles. Additional engineering with Luke Walton on Bear Attack's Shapes (2012), engineer on Justin Klunk's EP, and a shared Lestat's West bill (June 28, 2012).

Writer, producer, and engineer in Los Angeles. Additional engineering with Luke Walton on Bear Attack's Shapes (2012), engineer on Justin Klunk's EP, and a shared Lestat's West bill (June 28, 2012). Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Matias Mora. Status on site: partial. Matias Mora is a Los Angeles writer, producer, and instrumentalist (Resin8 Music). He, Luke Walton, and Leland Cox share the additional-engineering credit on Bear Attack's Shapes (Bandcamp, April 27, 2012). On June 28, 2012, the Lestat's West marquee billed AJ Peacox, Luke Walton, and Matias Mora. He engineered Justin Klunk – EP (released July 19, 2013), with Luke on guitar, and co-produced Klunk's second EP, Clarity (2016), which has no Luke credit. USC Today (March 10, 2016) lists him in Mora Mora, the 2013 popular-music class, with Cary Singer, Riley Knapp, and Nick Campbell. With Luke Walton: Bear Attack: Shapes (April 27, 2012) (Bandcamp · additional engineering: Leland Cox, Matias Mora, Luke Walton); Lestat's West (June 28, 2012) (marquee: AJ Peacox · Luke Walton · Matias Mora); Justin Klunk – EP (July 19, 2013) (Mora engineer · Luke guitar · Klunk sax · Gabe Rudner keys · Nick Campbell bass · Nate Laguzza drums · session photo posted February 13, 2014). Press on Matias Mora releases: Resin8 Music: Matias Mora, USC Today: Rising stars (March 10, 2016). Related hubs: Bear Attack, Justin Klunk, Cary Singer.
