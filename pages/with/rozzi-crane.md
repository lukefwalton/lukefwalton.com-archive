Rozzi Crane is connected to the Thornton School of Music, which Luke F. Walton graduated from. Rosalind Elizabeth Crane, Rozzi, was born May 2, 1991, in San Francisco. She entered the first Popular Music class in 2009, with Luke and Sam Wilkes (Wikipedia; Rolling Stone on that class). Their first tour together was a self-booked run up the California coast in the summer of 2010, San Diego to San Francisco. The Luke Walton Band played one bill. Her band was the same Walton lineup with Wilkes on bass instead of Nick Campbell. Campbell played bass on the Goodbye/Hello LP and the showcase clips. From those years: a Daily Trojan story on a midterm at the Grammy Museum Sound Stage, Crane singing and Walton taking a guitar solo on Earth, Wind and Fire's That's the Way of the World. Popular Music Showcase 2010 footage survives for Rosanna (Toto) and A Long Walk (Jill Scott), both with Campbell on bass and Logan Shrewsbury on drums. USC Thornton writes up her student years on its own. The University Chorus Holiday Concert program (November 21, 2010, 8:00 p.m., United University Church) lists Rozzi Crane, alto, and Luke Walton, tenor, on the same roster. After she left the school, Wikipedia records Lifetime syncs in her first year, background vocals for Don Henley and Sergio Mendes, Adam Levine's 222 Records in 2012, Maroon 5 tours (Wilkes later on bass, in 2015), and albums including Bad Together (2018). This site has no studio release they made together. The work with Luke is from 2009 and 2010.
