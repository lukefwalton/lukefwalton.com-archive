# With Noah Benardout

## Record

### With Noah Benardout

**Summary.** Singer, guitarist, and songwriter. Fronted Exist Elsewhere with Luke Walton, Brandon Woodward, and Nick Petrou (413, 2013); Luke and Brandon played in his band for his July 2012 Hard Rock Cafe Hollywood residency.

Singer, guitarist, and songwriter. Fronted Exist Elsewhere with Luke Walton, Brandon Woodward, and Nick Petrou (413, 2013); Luke and Brandon played in his band for his July 2012 Hard Rock Cafe Hollywood residency. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Noah Benardout. Status on site: partial. Noah Benardout sang and played guitar and keys in Exist Elsewhere. The band began as his backing band: in July 2012 he played a Friday residency at Hard Rock Cafe Hollywood, billed under his own name, with Luke Walton and Brandon Woodward in the band. GigTown dates Exist Elsewhere's birth to the last night of that residency, July 27, 2012. With Nick Petrou on bass (after Aaron Dowler), the band released 413 on August 2, 2013, including Tokyo, whose Kinect-shot video Andrew Gant directed. Luke and Brandon left to start FEiN; Noah kept Exist Elsewhere going with a new lineup into 2014. He later recorded as LOVR®. Noah was killed by a drunk driver on August 1, 2019. The Noah Benardout Foundation advocates DUI prevention in his memory. Please consider a donation to the foundation. With Luke Walton: Hard Rock Cafe Hollywood residency (July 2012) (billed as Noah Benardout · Fridays, July 6–27, 2012 · Luke Walton and Brandon Woodward in his band); Live on Isle of Wight Radio (recorded October 14, 2012 · Exist Elsewhere · Aaron Dowler bass); Macworld / Sonicbids iNDIE iNNOVATION (January 31, 2013) (Exist Elsewhere on the Main Stage · Noah speaking · Luke with guitar); 413 (August 2, 2013 · Exist Elsewhere · vocals, guitar, keys · with Luke, Brandon Woodward, Nick Petrou); Tokyo (413 · video directed by Andrew Gant); Exist Elsewhere (the band on /music/). Press on Noah Benardout releases: ReverbNation: Noah Benardout, The Noah Benardout Foundation, Donate: The Noah Benardout Foundation. Related hubs: Nick Petrou, Aaron Dowler, Andrew Gant.
