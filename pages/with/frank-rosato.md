### With Frank Rosato

Mixing and mastering engineer at Woodcliff Studios in Sherman Oaks, where Frank Rosato mixes. Frequent collaborator at Tiny Giant Recording, the Westlake Village studio Luke F. Walton and Brandon Woodward co-own, including FEiN’s Little Homes, Nina Francis’s Between Dreams, and Hammer City’s Heartquake Saga. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Frank Rosato. Status on site: complete. Frank Rosato mixes and masters at Woodcliff Studios in Sherman Oaks. Public copy calls him the studio's recording engineer. Other sessions, apart from Luke's catalog, include Susie Glaze's Live at McCabe's and Marty Friedman's Drama. On FEiN's Little Homes, the Discogs release (CD, May 31, 2016) says Tiny Giant Recording recorded it. Rosato mixed and mastered it at Woodcliff Studios in Sherman Oaks. The SoundCloud upload of Outro matches. On Nina Francis's Between Dreams, Bandcamp says she wrote it, Luke and Brandon Woodward produced it at Tiny Giant, and Rosato mixed and mastered it at Woodcliff. Most FEiN singles from 2017 to 2021 follow that same path. Bodies (October 2021) does not: Cary Singer mixed it and Riley Knapp mastered it, per Discogs. A YouTube copy of Forget I Said Anything ties him to Heartquake Saga as a Tiny Giant recording with a Rosato mix. That copy is a secondary source. On Exist Elsewhere's 413 (2013) the credit is wider: Rosato, Luke, and Brandon Woodward all engineered the EP, and Rosato mixed and mastered it. That is Luke's account. The song pages, including Tokyo, carry it. Outside this catalog, Muso.ai and other public credits put him on Barry Manilow, Spock's Beard and Pattern-Seeking Animals, Kolohe Kai, Latin Grammy-recognized ranchero sessions, and a long run of Woodcliff rock and pop dates. He engineered Gregg Bissonette's drums at Woodcliff on Jim Camacho's Looking for a Devil Among Angels, a Fernando Perdomo production. Luke talks about Frank and the Tiny Giant to Woodcliff path on Love Music More with Fernando Perdomo. Bissonette was Brandon Woodward's high-school drum teacher.
