### With Thumpasaurus

**Summary.** Los Angeles pop-funk band (USC alumni, like Luke) whose 2023 single Lipstick Makeup Luke Francis Walton reshaped as the Scoobert Doobert Edit (September 27, 2024, Beformer; about two minutes).

Los Angeles pop-funk band (USC alumni, like Luke) whose 2023 single Lipstick Makeup Luke Francis Walton reshaped as the Scoobert Doobert Edit (September 27, 2024, Beformer; about two minutes). Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Thumpasaurus. Status on site: partial. An edit credit, not a Scoobert Doobert original: the song is Thumpasaurus's. Lipstick Makeup (Scoobert Doobert Edit) (September 27, 2024, Beformer) is Walton's roughly two-minute edit of Thumpasaurus's March 2023 single, which the band framed as saying "fuck it" and letting your freak out at the dance party (CelebMix); Run The Trap heard dance-pop funk in the original. Scoobert Doobert edit: Lipstick Makeup (Scoobert Doobert Edit) (Sep 27, 2024 · Beformer · 2:00 edit of the March 2023 single · ISRC QZTBB2416130). Press on Thumpasaurus releases: Run The Trap: Lipstick Makeup, CelebMix: Lipstick Makeup.
