# With Andrew Gant

## Record

### With Andrew Gant

**Summary.** Director. Directed Exist Elsewhere's Kinect-shot Tokyo music video (2013) for Private School Entertainment.

Director. Directed Exist Elsewhere's Kinect-shot Tokyo music video (2013) for Private School Entertainment. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Andrew Gant. Status on site: partial. Andrew Gant directed the video for Tokyo, from Exist Elsewhere's 413, for Private School Entertainment in 2013, shooting the band with a Kinect, a DSLR, and a projector through RGBDToolkit. No Film School interviewed him about the rig in August 2013; he credits Sarah Oh with the lightman art. His portfolio says the project pushed his work toward narrative film and volumetric capture. With Luke Walton: Tokyo (music video) (2013 · Exist Elsewhere · director · Private School Entertainment); Tokyo: Kinect still (credit: Andrew Gant / Private School Entertainment); 413 (August 2, 2013 · the EP Tokyo is from). Press on Andrew Gant releases: Andrew Gant: Exist Elsewhere, No Film School: Creating a motion-capture video with a DSLR, Kinect, and projector (August 2013). Related hubs: Noah Benardout, Sarah Oh.
