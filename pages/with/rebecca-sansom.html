With Rebecca Sansom

Summary. Filmmaker, artist manager, and Blonde Records founder. Rebecca helped launch The Wavys inclusive music-industry awards and hosts Wavy Music News (originally Blonde Records Music News on Left Bank Magazine's YouTube). Luke Walton co-wrote The Wavys theme song with her; she edits Love Music More guest episodes and Love Music Why shorts.

Rebecca Autumn Sansom is a Nashville-born filmmaker and indie-music operator: Emmy-winning broadcast work, Blonde Artist Management / Blonde Records (founded 2015), and the MTV-esque weekly news format that became Wavy Music News after the pandemic-era Left Bank Live virtual festival with Left Bank Magazine and Bands do BK. With Scoobert Doobert: September 2020 Instagram promo for Blonde Records Music News lists @mr.scoobertdoobert among correspondents for the Left Bank Magazine YouTube broadcast, Luke's first appearance on Rebecca's music-news show. Little Hug nominated for EP of the Year at The Wavys 2021 (@wavyawards carousel, Sep 24, 2021).
