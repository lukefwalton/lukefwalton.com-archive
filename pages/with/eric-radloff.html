With Eric Radloff

Summary. Eric Radloff records as Okudaxij (earlier Scoobert credits: Gokudaxij; Bear Attack credits sometimes spell Erik). Bear Attack is his band. Featured vocalist and co-writer with Scoobert Doobert, including Happy Birthday and the Babidi & Gokudaxij tracks. Babidi on those tracks is Taylor Washington (J MESA), a different person. Artist site: okudaxij.bandcamp.com.

Eric Radloff is the person behind Okudaxij. Scoobert credits through 2020 usually spell the alias Gokudaxij. Streaming and the Happy Birthday video use Okudaxij. Bear Attack's Shapes credits sometimes spell Erik Radloff. Same person. Bear Attack is his band. It broke up; the music is still up. Luke worked all of Shapes (April 27, 2012): additional engineering, recording, and post production. The Bandcamp pane prints that as additional engineering. It is not a co-write. Babidi is Taylor Washington (J MESA), a different person. The two sing together on Adonde Eres and on several Dragon Ball $d tracks. Happy Birthday is Eric without Babidi. His artist site is Okudaxij on Bandcamp (Los Angeles). That page is his, not a Scoobert release page. On Fresh Fruit (FEiN × Lara Johnston, 2015) the SoundCloud credit block lists Eric as hype man. Before USC he played piano with Luke. The deleted Wikipedia article lists him as a past member, on piano. Before those credits, the University Chorus Holiday Concert program (November 21, 2010, 8:00 p.m., United University Church) lists Eric Radloff and Luke Walton together in the tenor section.
