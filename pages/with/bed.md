Tokyo band bed. Luke remixed mother ship, then co-wrote, co-produced, and mixed Kare Wa 3.0, the opening theme of the Fuji TV drama HEART ATTACK. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with bed. Status on site: complete. bed play a kind of post-punk that wants to be a rave. UNE, SINJI, JONEU, and SHUN-1 started the band in 2022. GATA and Crack describe the late sets in Tokyo: a heavy bass, a big riff, a techno kick, one song running into the next. They threw parties at Shibuya clubasia, the band plus DJs, and in 2023 they reportedly filled a warehouse in Shin-Kiba that holds about 600 people. Crack also describes a world they call 2052: blacked-out graphics, green flyers, the songs and the stage treated as one thing. The singles start with APOLOGIZE in July 2022 and run through Kare Wa, Michael Mann, and mother ship in April 2023, then 130 and ISEI. Archives, dated May 27, 2023, is the long release. In EYESCREAM the band refuse to call it a first album made for tours and festivals. They call it a recording of the live show. For HEART ATTACK, Spincoaster and SENSA say the director, Takeshi Maruyama, first wanted the live version, Kare Wa 2.0, under the opening titles. bed recorded a new 3.0, and that became the TV theme. Luke co-wrote it, co-produced it, and mixed it. In September 2025 the series won ContentAsia's silver award for best drama series made in Asia for a regional or international market. That prize is the show's. Crack later reported a London show in 2025, then Forty Miles and UK dates in 2026.
