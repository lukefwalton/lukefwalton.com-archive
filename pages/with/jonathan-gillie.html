Played guitar in Mannequin, the band he and Luke co-founded with Zach Freeman at La Costa Canyon (about 2006–2008). Co-author of The Signatories. On Surmado's advisory board. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Jonathan Gillie. Status on site: partial. Zach Freeman played drums, and Peter Rosenbaum joined on bass. The first practice was Gillie, Luke, and Freeman in Luke's parents' garage, around 2007, with no bass player yet. They came in second at the school's Battle of the Bands. The set included a Katy Perry cover. That's how Luke remembers it. A Wikipedia article on The Luke Walton Band listed the same lineup and the same second place. It was deleted July 16, 2026; the June 18, 2026 capture is what remains. Wikidata doesn't name the members. No school record has turned up, and no recording of the band has either. Gillie played bass at Luke's first gig, the only show Rock I IV V ever played. Luke had a 103 degree fever. The song he remembers is "Crazy Little Thing Called Love." He and Luke created cawleen, the fictional artist in The Signatories. His site is jonathangillie.com. Music with Luke Walton: Rock I IV V (Luke's first gig, and the only time the band played.
