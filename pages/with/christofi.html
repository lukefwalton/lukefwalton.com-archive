With Christofi

Summary. FEiN collaborator: co-writer and co-billed artist on Marry You On the Dance Floor (Christofi & FEiN, February 9, 2016), 1,079,462 Spotify plays as of October 5, 2026, and #2 on Spotify’s Mexico Viral 50 on May 21, 2016. Named with FEiN’s The Disco in the January 2017 ReverbNation interview.

FEiN collaborator: co-writer and co-billed artist on Marry You On the Dance Floor (Christofi & FEiN, February 9, 2016), 1,079,462 Spotify plays as of October 5, 2026, and #2 on Spotify’s Mexico Viral 50 on May 21, 2016. Named with FEiN’s The Disco in the January 2017 ReverbNation interview. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Christofi. Status on site: partial. Also credited as: Michael Christofi. Christofi’s own releases are not on this site. His engineering credits here appear under his full name, Michael Christofi. Marry You On the Dance Floor (February 9, 2016) is a Christofi & FEiN single: written by Luke Francis Walton and Brandon Michael Woodward with Christofi, released on Christofi’s SoundCloud, with Brian Robert Jones on bass. On October 5, 2026, Spotify showed 1,079,462 plays. A May 21, 2016 FEiN Facebook post (screenshot) shows it at #2 on Spotify’s Mexico Viral 50, ahead of Justin Timberlake’s CAN’T STOP THE FEELING! It sits in FEiN’s 2016 collaboration run beside Boomerang (Nina Francis); it is not on Little Homes. The ReverbNation Blog interview (January 27, 2017) names Christofi and The Disco together as a FEiN collab, with Luke on the “fourth writer” a collaboration brings in; the song page itself credits FEiN featuring Mike Funk. This site’s Little Homes-era Q&A page names Christofi, with Embody, in the duo’s ongoing collaboration series. Sessions: a February 22, 2016 FEiN Facebook throwback (screenshot) reads recording #Grownupz on an ironing board with Christofi, the #Grownupz vocal takes tracked in Gary Sinise’s garage. The Little Homes liner notes credit additional engineering by Michael Christofi at LMU, the August 2014 drum day where the album began, and list him in the special thanks; FEiN recorded the album themselves at Tiny Giant, so Luke Walton and Brandon Woodward engineered it too, with Christofi’s work the additional credit. Collaborations with FEiN: Marry You On the Dance Floor (May 2016 · Christofi & FEiN · co-write · #2 Spotify Mexico Viral 50 · Brian Robert Jones, bass). Sessions: #Grownupz (Feb 2016 · vocal takes tracked in Gary Sinise’s garage (FEiN Facebook: “with Christofi”)); Little Homes (2016 LP · additional engineering at LMU, beside FEiN’s own · special thanks). Related hubs: FEiN hub (Luke Walton + Brandon Woodward), With Brian Robert Jones (bass on Marry You On the Dance Floor).
