Blues trio at La Costa Canyon High School, about 2008–2009, co-founded by Luke Walton (guitar, vocals), Zach Freeman (drums), and Jordan Sorokin (bass). Won the school Battle of the Bands and recorded The Other Side in 2009. Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Blue Suburbia. Status on site: complete. Mostly covers: Eric Clapton, John Mayer, and songs in that line, plus a few of Luke's, chiefly Preschool. They recorded The Other Side, about seventeen minutes, in the summer of 2009, then left for college. The archived discography lists the EP as Luke Walton featuring Blue Suburbia, and names Freeman on drums and Sorokin on bass. The 2009 Coast News profile confirms the win. A Wikipedia article on The Luke Walton Band listed the same trio. It was deleted July 16, 2026; the June 18, 2026 capture is what remains. Wikidata doesn't name the members. The other rounds are in the catalog. Releases: Soundboard Studio West (August 20, 2009, Rancho Bernardo. Jordan Sorokin, Zach Freeman, and Luke Walton, giving notes to the mix engineer. Press on Blue Suburbia releases: The Coast News: Six Months to Make It (2009), Charitable Chords: Know Your Rights ("Luke Walton (Blue Suburbia) — Preschool").
