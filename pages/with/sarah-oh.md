### With Sarah Oh

**Summary.** Artist. Album art for Exist Elsewhere's 413 and the lightman concept and art in its Tokyo video (2013); zine cover, layout, and album art for FEiN's Little Homes (2016).

Artist. Album art for Exist Elsewhere's 413 and the lightman concept and art in its Tokyo video (2013); zine cover, layout, and album art for FEiN's Little Homes (2016). Collaborator hub for Luke F. Walton and Scoobert Doobert credits with Sarah Oh. Status on site: partial. Sarah Oh made the album art for Exist Elsewhere's 413 (August 2, 2013) and the lightman concept and art in the Kinect-shot Tokyo video, which Andrew Gant directed; the band's Vimeo upload and Gant's No Film School interview both credit her. Three years later she made the zine cover and layout and the album art for FEiN's Little Homes (May 31, 2016), per the FEiN Times Issue #3 liner notes, which also thank her.
