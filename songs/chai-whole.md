WHOLE follows the Sub Pop remix on [*WINK TOGETHER*](https://www.subpop.com/releases/chai/wink_together). [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/news/chai-announce-new-remix-ep-share-confidence-man-remix-listen/) lists Miracle (Scoobert Doobert Remix) on that EP. On July 28, 2022, [@wavyawards nominated WHOLE for Collaboration of the Year](https://www.instagram.com/p/CgjwOjpsCcT/).

[NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/chais-dancey-new-single-whole-is-a-plea-for-empathy-3137597) names Walton as producer and names the NHK series Koisenu Futari. [Qetic](https://qetic.jp/music/chai-210112/421668/) (January 12, 2022) credits California-based Scoobert Doobert with the sound production on まるごと. [SPICE](https://spice.eplus.jp/articles/297572) (January 13, 2022) is Spotify's New Music Wednesday companion for that week. まるごと is in the January 12 selection, and the same piece says it was the cover of Edge!. That is a magazine note, not a Spotify for Artists email. [Paste](https://www.pastemagazine.com/music/chai/new-single-whole) and [FLOOD](https://floodmagazine.com/97933/listen-chai-whole/) covered the single the same week. [Sony Music Japan](https://www.sonymusic.co.jp/artist/chai/info/536883) documents まるごと as the drama theme, with Scoobert Doobert on sound production. [Sub Pop Mega Mart](https://megamart.subpop.com/products/chai_whole) names Scoobert Doobert as producer.

[Tower Records Japan](https://tower.jp/item/5579468) credits the arrangement on the ジャジャーン cut to CHAI and Scoobert Doobert. Lyrics are Yuuki's. Composition is Mana and Kana's. [SoundBetter](https://soundbetter.com/profiles/489488-scoobert-doobert) lists producer, arranger, and mixing engineer. YouTube's [official audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ozjOkzdS5u0) and [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n-fNkTFGxfA) carry the public metadata.

CHAI's drummer [Yuna](https://www.instagram.com/chai.yuna/) posted a drum-recording reel for まるごと on January 12, 2022, the day before release ([#yunadrum](https://www.instagram.com/reel/CYoXemyBbwe/)). A screenshot is on [Photos](/music/#photos). [Anthony Fantano](/with/anthony-fantano/) reacted to WHOLE in a [fan-captured clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IfQ2d07vus4). The clip is also on [Selected Videos](/music/#selected-videos) and [Videos with CHAI](/with/chai/#videos).

The series, not the theme song, later received major Japanese television prizes: the [59th Galaxy Award Television Division Special Prize](https://www.houkon.jp/galaxy-award/%E7%AC%AC59%E5%9B%9E%EF%BC%882021%E5%B9%B4%E5%BA%A6%EF%BC%89/) (the jury called it a groundbreaking work in drama history) and an [Excellence Award at the 77th Agency for Cultural Affairs Arts Festival](https://www.bunka.go.jp/koho_hodo_oshirase/hodohappyo/93808501.html). Writer [Erika Yoshida won the 40th Kuniko Mukōda Prize](https://tokyonews.co.jp/mukouda/prize40/) for the script. Those awards are the drama's and Yoshida's; Walton produced the theme. Hosted transcripts: [Sony Music Japan](/press-kit/evidence/#chai-whole-sony-music-japan), [Sub Pop Mega Mart](/press-kit/evidence/#chai-whole-sub-pop), [Tower Records](/press-kit/evidence/#chai-jajarn-tower-records), [Galaxy Award](/press-kit/evidence/#koisenu-futari-galaxy-award).

**Final live:** CHAI disbanded after their **March 12, 2024** show at **EX THEATER ROPPONGI**. [Sony Music Japan](https://www.sonymusic.co.jp/artist/chai/info/563144) documents the first Blu-ray **『We The CHAI Tour! FINAL ～NEO KAWAII IS FOREVER♡～』** (**July 3, 2024** · SIXL-3): full last-show video (~106 min, 22 songs) plus documentary. **Track 11: まるごと (WHOLE)**, Walton production. Track 3: [ラブじゃん](/songs/chai-thats-love/) (also Walton). Full setlist on [Final Live](/with/chai/#final-live).
