Out of My Mind (FEiN X Lara Johnston) went up on SoundCloud on June 28, 2015. The upload calls it the first collaborative release in the FEiN Times series, “FEiN Times Lara Johnston,” and points to a free download. FEiN produced and recorded it at Tiny Giant. Frank Rosato mixed and mastered it at Woodcliff Studios. The archive master matches that upload: 4:27, tagged Lara Johnston, 2015.

It is not on Little Homes. The later studio collaboration is Fresh Fruit (FEiN x Lara Johnston), uploaded September 15, 2015. Walton had already toured as guitarist and vocalist with Lara Johnston, opening for the Doobie Brothers and Gregg Allman. She now performs as Lara Lucille.

A morning-after ballad built on domestic residue: cold sheets, brush my teeth lonely, the coffee made for two. The pre-chorus accepts the ending but not the manner of it (maybe we were bound to break / but not like this), which is the whole tension the chorus keeps circling.

The hook is a stuck loop. My heart keeps slipping back in time, no matter what I do, and the title command (get out of my mind) repeats because it never works. The bridge names the trap as paralysis and envy, memories keep me paralyzed, the ex moving on while the narrator can't. It sits with Boomerang (long-distance motion) and Roadtrip (doesn't really matter): a FEiN pop ballad with none of the Little Homes satire.
