Content

Track seven on the Bandcamp $WAMI$ (LP) (May 9, 2018, ~1:12). Sits between I Want Yu Yu (feat. Babidi) and Jeff Sessions (Good People Don't Smoke Marijuana). The title points at Sappho's surviving fragments: leftover lines as the concept, collage LP as the form.

Bandcamp-only. Spotify's $WAMI$ drops this slot and puts Essence of You in the mid-album instrumental lane instead. Venue split on the album page.

Luke Francis Walton wrote, played, tracked, and mixed. See Catalog · $WAMI$ album notes
