Track seven on the [Bandcamp *$WAMI$ (LP)*](/albums/wami/) (**May 9, 2018**, ~**1:12**). Sits between [*I Want Yu Yu (feat. Babidi)*](/songs/i-want-yu-yu-feat-babidi/) and [*Jeff Sessions (Good People Don't Smoke Marijuana)*](/songs/jeff-sessions-good-people-dont-smoke-marijuana-feat-beauregard-the-third/). The title points at [Sappho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sappho)'s surviving fragments: leftover lines as the concept, collage LP as the form.

**Bandcamp-only.** Spotify's *$WAMI$* drops this slot and puts [*Essence of You*](/songs/essence-of-you/) in the mid-album instrumental lane instead. Venue split on the [album page](/albums/wami/).

Luke Francis Walton wrote, played, tracked, and mixed. See [Catalog](/catalog/) · [*$WAMI$* album notes](/albums/wami/)
