Content

Ho Ho Ho (Ozempic), standalone holiday meme single, separate from A Very Doobert Christmas, Vol. 1 (2023). Catalog lists it on an indiemono holiday compilation (2025), title pairs Santa’s ho ho ho with Ozempic / GLP-1 cultural moment as punchline. The lyric stacks that with Amazon Prime drones, AI Rudolph, iPhone 30 sleigh bells, and ho ho ho inflation.

Not part of the MÖBIUS LP cycle. One of Scoobert’s one-off holiday drops, with Merry Christmas from the Beach and the Christmas covers.
