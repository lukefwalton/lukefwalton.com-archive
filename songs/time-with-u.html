Content

Track two on I, out ahead of the LP as a single. Nagamag heard catchy, harmonic bedroom/lo-fi pop.

Spotify worldwide New Music Friday, January 5, 2024. Genius transcribes that week's playlist and lists Scoobert Doobert – time with u between Henrik and guardin. Purple Melon (January 11, 2024) names the same add and All New Indie. The same two worldwide New Music Friday adds are this song and I'm an Idiot (September 3, 2021). ADNKDN (March 3, 2024) filed it as a 2024 best-song candidate and noted the CHAI remix. Spotify for Artists emailed the New Music Friday add the next morning, January 6, 2024 at 9:06 a.m. (4,194,861 followers), and added the song to Bedroom Pop on January 13, 2024 at 4:33 p.m. (1,104,107 followers).

Blue Bottle Coffee Japan, November 15, 2024. ＃MUSIC WITH COFFEE VOL.119： Autumn Stroll lists Scoobert Doobert, time with u on the published playlist.

The opener names the bit: I think my phone is dead but I might leave it that way. The chorus completes the thought, and it isn't "I miss you" or "I want you," it's nothing on the internet that's shiny and new. On a travel record full of Shinkansen and PCH, this is the song that picks the sofa, stacking nothing in particular until nothing sounds like a destination.

Also on Catalog as 2024 single.
