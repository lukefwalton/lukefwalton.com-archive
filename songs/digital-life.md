Track 2 on [*Live on Isle of Wight Radio*](/albums/live-on-isle-of-wight-radio/) (**November 1, 2012** on [Bandcamp](https://existelsewhere.bandcamp.com/album/live-on-isle-of-wight-radio), 3:50). The session this site dates to **October 14, 2012**, recorded for Isle of Wight Radio at the House of Rock in Santa Monica. **Aaron Dowler** on bass, before Nick Petrou. Not on [*413*](/albums/413/).

The master is free here: [MP3](/audio/digital-life.mp3) · [WAV](/audio/digital-life.wav). Pro Tools bounce dated **October 18, 2012**. Exist Elsewhere also played *Digital Life* at the [2013 Macworld/iWorld iNDIE iNNOVATION Championship](/albums/413/). [Guild Guitars](https://web.archive.org/web/20210127205328/https://guildguitars.com/exist-elsewhere-records-acoustic-version-of-the-great-beyond-and-digital-life/) later posted an acoustic version from this record.
