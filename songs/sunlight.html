Content

Track three on MÖB, the turn toward warmth after Stories' head-loop. The DistroKid credit pane lists music and lyrics as Luke Francis Walton, BMI 00579587572. Producer, mixing, recording, drums, bass, guitar, synthesizer, and singing are billed as Scoobert Doobert. Riley Knapp mastered it. Ear to the Ground Music singled it out for bouncy funk energy, which fits its job: the album trying an answer to its own anxiety. The verses are a summer inventory (popsicle picnic, mango with Tajín, margarita number two) and the chorus is a prescription repeated until it sticks: imma be in the sunlight, throw a beer back, savor it.

The line that carries it is nobody needs me. That's the freedom move: permission to exist without being on call, not martyrdom. On an LP shadowed by illness and dread, Sunlight is the choice to step into the light anyway. It pairs with Tired of the Sunshine on Moonlight Beach as the push-pull on California brightness. Here the vote is for the sun.
