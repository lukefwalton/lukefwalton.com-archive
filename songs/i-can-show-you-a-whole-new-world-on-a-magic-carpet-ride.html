Content

Track fifteen on the Bandcamp $WAMI$ (LP) (May 9, 2018, ~2:07). Sits between Shaggy Connections (Louis Cole drums) and Mother of Exile. The title borrows the Aladdin pitch line as collage humor, not a streaming cover credit: $WAMI$ treating cartoon and Disney language the same way it treats DBZ and Scooby.

Bandcamp-only. Spotify jumps from Shaggy Connections to Mother of Exile. Venue split on the album page.

Luke Francis Walton wrote, played, tracked, and mixed. See Catalog · $WAMI$ album notes
