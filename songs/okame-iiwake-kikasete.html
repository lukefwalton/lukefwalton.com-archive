Content

言いわけきかせて (Iiwake Kikasete): OKAME single, digital release July 25, 2026 (Spotify · Apple Music · OTOTOY, KODO RECORDS, catalog KODO-DIGI-002, 2:20). It follows DEBUT (February 4, 2026).

Theme song for the short anime きょうもカクレチカクカ・ビン. The official MV description and MANAKANA set episode 1 on July 25, 2026 (知覚過敏の日), with 10 episodes planned, on TikTok and elsewhere. Cast named there: カクレチカクカ・ビン, こわいタコ, わかいクジラ. Rooftop describes it as the everyday lives of slightly sensitive fish (ちょっと敏感なお魚たち). The Third Strategy (The Third Animation) is the company behind the short; the homepage names the series and the tie-up single. Uta-Net lists the anime with the song (作詞 MANA, 作曲 OKAME). JOYSOUND reports the same theme-song credit.

The official MV is a separate video from the anime clips. YouTube credits chao! as director, concept, and storyboard; hair, makeup, and styling by OKAME. Also on Selected Videos.

Luke Francis Walton mixed the single. Spotify’s public credits list OKAME as composer and MANA as lyricist, and do not yet name a mixing engineer.
