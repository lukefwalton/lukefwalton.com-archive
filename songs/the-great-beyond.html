Track 1 on Live on Isle of Wight Radio (November 1, 2012 on Bandcamp, 4:34). The session this site dates to October 14, 2012, recorded for Isle of Wight Radio at the House of Rock in Santa Monica. Aaron Dowler on bass, before Nick Petrou. Not on 413.

The master is free here: MP3 · WAV. Pro Tools bounce dated October 18, 2012. Guild Guitars later posted an acoustic version from this record.
