Track ten on the [Bandcamp *$WAMI$ (LP)*](/albums/wami/) (**May 9, 2018**), **instrumental** sequel to [*My Scoobert Snack*](/songs/my-scoobert-snack/). Bandcamp length is **~1:11**; Spotify lists **2:01**. On Bandcamp it sits between [*Spice Up the Bedroom With Spirit Cuffs*](/songs/spice-up-the-bedroom-with-spirit-cuffs/) and [*Adonde Eres*](/songs/adonde-eres-feat-babidi-gokudaxij/). Spotify keeps the cut and drops the Bandcamp-only interludes around it.

The [$WAMI$ album notes](/albums/wami/) name *Scoobert Snack, Pt. 2*, *What a Velma, What a Night (Quickie)*, and *Mother of Exile* as the tracks that revisit the debut's world. Snack branding, a recurring joke title, predates the post-pandemic [*Big Hug*](/albums/big-hug/) LP.
