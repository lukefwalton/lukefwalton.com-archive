**The Way We Move** (2018): **Accidental Muse** and **Tamtam** single from the 2018 run, with [Summertime Feeling](/songs/accidental-muse-summertime-feeling/), [Home](/songs/accidental-muse-home/), [Chill Vibes](/songs/accidental-muse-chill-vibes/), [Weakness](/songs/accidental-muse-weakness/), and [Light and Dark](/songs/accidental-muse-light-and-dark/). **Tamtam sang** on every Accidental Muse song. Luke Walton's account. **Luke Francis Walton**: composer, co-writer, recording engineer, mix engineer; keys and guitar. [York Calling](https://yorkcalling.co.uk/2018/10/15/discovery-accidental-muse-x-tamtam/) (Jane Howkins, October 15, 2018) reviews the track and spells her name Tantam.
