Track eleven on the Bandcamp $WAMI$ (LP) (May 9, 2018, ~0:50). Sits between Scoobert Snack, Pt. 2 and Adonde Eres. Title-as-the-bit: bedroom-pop innuendo in the Encinitas bathrobe collage, same joke-title lane as Vegeta Is Bulma's Bad Boi.

Bandcamp-only. Spotify keeps Scoobert Snack, Pt. 2 and Adonde Eres and skips this interlude. Venue split on the album page.

Luke Francis Walton wrote, played, tracked, and mixed. See Catalog · $WAMI$ album notes
