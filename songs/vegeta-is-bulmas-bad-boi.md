Track three on the [Bandcamp *$WAMI$ (LP)*](/albums/wami/) (**May 9, 2018**), about **eighteen seconds**. **Louis Cole** sits in on the drum-and-bass moment, same kit credit as [*Shaggy Connections*](/songs/shaggy-connections/). The title is the whole gag: [Vegeta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegeta) as [Bulma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulma)'s bad boi, domesticated prince as collage punchline before [*Dragon Ball $d*](/albums/dragon-ball-d/) turns that cosmology into a hip-hopera.

This cut is **Bandcamp-only**. Spotify's *$WAMI$* skips it (and the other Bandcamp-only interludes) and jumps from [*Phone Plan*](/songs/phone-plan/) to [*Friends*](/songs/friends/). Venue split documented on the [album page](/albums/wami/).
