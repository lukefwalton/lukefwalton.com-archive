Track twelve on the [Bandcamp *$WAMI$ (LP)*](/albums/wami/), **Adonde Eres** (Spanish: *where are you*) with **Babidi** and **Gokudaxij** on featured vocals, between [*Spice Up the Bedroom With Spirit Cuffs*](/songs/spice-up-the-bedroom-with-spirit-cuffs/) and [*What a Velma, What a Night*](/songs/what-a-velma-what-a-night/). Gokudaxij also appears on later [*Dragon Ball $d*](/albums/dragon-ball-d/) cuts (*Prince of All Saiyans*, *Namek Has Dragon Balls*, etc.); here the pair is still in collage mode on the free 2018 LP.

Luke Francis Walton produced the session. Babidi / J MESA appears on *$WAMI$* and again on the 2020 hip-hopera. See [With J MESA](/with/j-mesa/).
