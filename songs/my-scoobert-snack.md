Standalone **instrumental** single (**2018**), before [*$WAMI$*](/albums/wami/) and before the character had a fully built world around him. Track two on [*Finding $D*](/albums/finding-d-remastered/). The name does the work: Scoobert Doobert is already a Scooby-Doo-adjacent alias. *Scoobert* is what you call a scooby, *snack* is what he wants: so **My Scoobert Snack** is both a self-description and the first installment of a bit. The joke continued as the **instrumental** [*Scoobert Snack, Pt. 2*](/songs/scoobert-snack-pt-2/), which landed on [*$WAMI$*](/albums/wami/) at Bandcamp track ten, sandwiched between [*Spice Up the Bedroom With Spirit Cuffs*](/songs/spice-up-the-bedroom-with-spirit-cuffs/) and [*Adonde Eres*](/songs/adonde-eres-feat-babidi-gokudaxij/).

By 2018, *snack* was also peak internet-speak for someone attractive, which Scoobert wears without explaining. The cut lives in the pre-WAMI zone: looser, less conceptually dense than the LP, but audibly the same person. No lyrics on instrumental pages.
