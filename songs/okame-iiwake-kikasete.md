**言いわけきかせて** (*Iiwake Kikasete*): OKAME single, digital release **July 25, 2026** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/64D27yKpPCdVtjl83OzTk6) · [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/song/iiwakekikasete/6790735865) · [OTOTOY](https://ototoy.jp/_/default/p/4038875), KODO RECORDS, catalog **KODO-DIGI-002**, 2:20). It follows [***DEBUT***](/albums/okame-debut/) (February 4, 2026).

Theme song for the short anime **きょうもカクレチカクカ・ビン**. The [official MV description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fiCtZ-SNHzc) and [MANAKANA](https://manakana.bitfan.id/contents/413965) set episode 1 on **July 25, 2026** (知覚過敏の日), with **10 episodes** planned, on TikTok and elsewhere. Cast named there: カクレチカクカ・ビン, こわいタコ, わかいクジラ. [Rooftop](https://rooftop1976.com/news/2026/08/04181000.php) describes it as the everyday lives of slightly sensitive fish (ちょっと敏感なお魚たち). [The Third Strategy](https://thirdstrategy.co.jp/) (The Third Animation) is the company behind the short; the homepage names the series and the tie-up single. [Uta-Net](https://www.uta-net.com/song/397322/) lists the anime with the song (作詞 MANA, 作曲 OKAME). [JOYSOUND](https://news.joysound.com/article/683716) reports the same theme-song credit.

The [official MV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fiCtZ-SNHzc) is a separate video from the anime clips. YouTube credits **chao!** as director, concept, and storyboard; hair, makeup, and styling by OKAME. Also on [Selected Videos](/music/#selected-videos).

Luke Francis Walton mixed the single. Spotify’s public credits list **OKAME** as composer and **MANA** as lyricist, and do not yet name a mixing engineer.

No Scoobert lyrics. See [With OKAME](/with/okame/) · [Catalog](/catalog/#production)
