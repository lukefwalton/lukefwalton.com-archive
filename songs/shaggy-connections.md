Track fourteen on the [Bandcamp *$WAMI$ (LP)*](/albums/wami/), after [*What a Velma, What a Night*](/songs/what-a-velma-what-a-night/) and before [*I Can Show You A Whole New World (on a Magic Carpet Ride)*](/songs/i-can-show-you-a-whole-new-world-on-a-magic-carpet-ride/) (Bandcamp-only; Spotify jumps to [*Mother of Exile*](/songs/mother-of-exile/)). Bandcamp length is **~0:47**; Spotify lists **3:58**. [**$WAMI$ album notes**](/albums/wami/) credit **Louis Cole** on the drum-and-bass moments here and on [*Vegeta Is Bulma's Bad Boi*](/songs/vegeta-is-bulmas-bad-boi/): one of the few outside players on an LP Luke otherwise tracked alone in a bathrobe.

“Connections” reads as a network pun; it sits in the late-album run of the collage without being a featured-vocal cut, more groove interlude with guest drums than hip-hopera.
