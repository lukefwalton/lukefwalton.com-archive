Content

Summertime Feeling is an Accidental Muse × Tamtam single from July 2018, the debut ahead of the other five. Tamtam sang on it, as she did on every Accidental Muse song. Luke Walton's account. Glamglare (Elke Nominikat, July 12, 2018) calls it the debut and names neither of them. The person behind the name is Luke Walton, billed as ax on the project. The piece says the person behind the name kept it hidden, was based in Los Angeles, and had toured as a hired guitarist for the Doobie Brothers, Gregg Allman, and Heart, with Daft Punk's Alive 2007 as the spark. The Doobie Brothers and Gregg Allman lines match the known support dates, guitar in Lara Johnston's band. Heart is only in this piece.

The same article quotes the artist: Summertime Feeling is about leaving the chaos of the world behind for a moment to escape into the bliss that only music can bring.

Spotify lists the recording under Accidental Muse. A YouTube teaser under that name was titled "Summertime Feeling - Coming July 6th!"

No Scoobert lyrics. See With Tamtam.
