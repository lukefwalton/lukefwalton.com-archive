San Diego Super Chargers Theme / Fight Song REMIX is on the Scoobert Doobert channel. YouTube dates the upload November 3, 2020. It runs 2:25. The description says it is his take on the San Diego Chargers song.

Beats and Brews, Episode 1 is a 15-minute homebrew video from the same day that also plays the remix. Those views belong to the episode.

YouTube only. Same San Diego jingle run as Let's Go Padres.
