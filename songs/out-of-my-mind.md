**Out of My Mind (FEiN X Lara Johnston)** went up on [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/feinmusic/feinxlara) on **June 28, 2015**. The upload calls it the first collaborative release in the FEiN Times series, “FEiN Times Lara Johnston,” and points to a [free download](https://feinmusic.toneden.io/spotlight/feinxlara). FEiN produced and recorded it at Tiny Giant. [Frank Rosato](/with/frank-rosato/) mixed and mastered it at Woodcliff Studios. The archive master matches that upload: 4:27, tagged Lara Johnston, 2015.

It is not on [*Little Homes*](/albums/little-homes-fein/). The later studio collaboration is [**Fresh Fruit (FEiN x Lara Johnston)**](/songs/fresh-fruit/), uploaded **September 15, 2015**. Walton had already toured as guitarist and vocalist with Lara Johnston, opening for the Doobie Brothers and Gregg Allman. She now performs as **Lara Lucille**.

A morning-after ballad built on domestic residue: cold sheets, *brush my teeth lonely*, the coffee made for two. The pre-chorus accepts the ending but not the manner of it (*maybe we were bound to break / but not like this*), which is the whole tension the chorus keeps circling.

The hook is a stuck loop. *My heart keeps slipping back in time, no matter what I do*, and the title command (*get out of my mind*) repeats because it never works. The bridge names the trap as paralysis and envy, *memories keep me paralyzed*, the ex moving on while the narrator can't. It sits with [*Boomerang*](/songs/boomerang/) (long-distance motion) and [*Roadtrip*](/songs/roadtrip/) (*doesn't really matter*): a FEiN pop ballad with none of the *Little Homes* satire.
