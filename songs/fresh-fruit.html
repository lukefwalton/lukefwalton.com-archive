Fresh Fruit (FEiN x Lara Johnston): a FRESH AND FUNKY end-of-summer collaboration (SoundCloud, uploaded September 15, 2015 · 3:37). Public SoundCloud credit block:

Brian Robert Jones: bass (SoundCloud: Brian Jones) · Eric Radloff: hype man · Brandon Woodward: drums/percussion/keys · Luke Walton: guitar/keys · Lara Johnston: vocals · produced and recorded by FEiN at Tiny Giant · Frank Rosato: mixing/mastering (Woodcliff Studios).

Walton toured as guitarist/vocalist with Lara Johnston (Doobie Brothers, Gregg Allman) before this studio collab (see Lara Johnston; she now performs as Lara Lucille: Facebook). Not on Little Homes; a FEiN collab alongside Boomerang (with FEiN) and Remember Us (Embody / Armada).
