Track fifteen on the [Bandcamp *$WAMI$ (LP)*](/albums/wami/) (**May 9, 2018**, **~2:07**). Sits between [*Shaggy Connections*](/songs/shaggy-connections/) (Louis Cole drums) and [*Mother of Exile*](/songs/mother-of-exile/). The title borrows the *Aladdin* pitch line as collage humor, not a streaming cover credit: *$WAMI$* treating cartoon and Disney language the same way it treats DBZ and Scooby.

**Bandcamp-only.** Spotify jumps from *Shaggy Connections* to *Mother of Exile*. Venue split on the [album page](/albums/wami/).

Luke Francis Walton wrote, played, tracked, and mixed. See [Catalog](/catalog/) · [*$WAMI$* album notes](/albums/wami/)
