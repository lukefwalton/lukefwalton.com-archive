Content

CHAI Japan single ラブじゃん（ That's Love） came out January 18, 2023, the same day Sony released the Japan-only EP 『ジャジャーン』 (Sony Music Japan). Walton’s credit is produced (played and mixed on the release as well). Official release hub: chaiband.lnk.to/LOVEJAN. Official MV on YouTube, also on Selected Videos.

Tower Records Japan lists the Sony EP 『ジャジャーン』 (SICX-184, January 18, 2023). Track metadata credits 編曲 on ラブじゃん to CHAI and Scoobert Doobert. Lyrics are ユウキ (CHAI); composition is マナ and カナ. The same shared arrangement credit is on まるごと and 夢のはなし. SURPRISE and HERO JOURNEY on that EP name other arrangers. Hosted transcript: Sources.

Not a U.S. release. The single is on Spotify Japan as ラブじゃん and does not appear in a U.S. Spotify search. On October 5, 2026, Spotify Japan showed 276,740 plays. The count moves. Let's Love is a separate CHAI anime-theme release.

Part of the post-WINK TOGETHER production run alongside WHOLE (track 11 まるごと on the final Blu-ray), 夢のはなし (MY DREAM), and だれかおるやろ feat. CHAI (CHAI × Shingo Murakami). Final live: track 3 on 『We The CHAI Tour! FINAL ～NEO KAWAII IS FOREVER♡～』: Mar 12, 2024 EX THEATER ROPPONGI · Blu-ray Jul 3, 2024. Full setlist: Final Live. More: With CHAI.
