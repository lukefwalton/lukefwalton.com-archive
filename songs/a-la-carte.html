Content

Scoobert Doobert cover of Okudaxij’s “A La Carte.” Track 4, 2:16, on Friends Covering Friends Vol. II (Z Tapes, Bratislava, catalog ZT196), released September 12, 2021. Digital only, name-your-price, Bandcamp. The page calls it a compilation of friends covering friends. Mastering and track order by Etai. Artwork by Nathaniel Kirch. Digital profits go to Manny of Citrus City Records after Hurricane Ida flooded his merch.

The same album’s track 15 is the reverse cover: Okudaxij (Eric Radloff) singing Scoobert’s Coca Cola, 2:58, Bandcamp.

Space Is The Place listed Scoobert Doobert, “A La Carte,” on the September 30, 2021 playlist (Mixcloud archive).
