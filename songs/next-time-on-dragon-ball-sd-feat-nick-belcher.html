Content

Next Time on Dragon Ball SD (feat. Nick Belcher) is the same DBZ SD trailer idea as Aaahh!!! Real Monsters, with Nick Belcher screaming over it. Not a lyric. The Bandcamp $WAMI$ closer is the instrumental Next Time on Dragon Ball SD (same recording as the Finding $D closer); this is the Spotify-album closer instead.

Nick Belcher: lead vocalist of Hammer City / The Hammers (Heartquake Saga EP, 2016), also features across Dragon Ball $d (Vegeta's Lament, Ally to Good, My Dad Sucks, etc.). This cut predates the full hip-hopera as a standalone scream feature, not as a sung verse.

Not on the Dragon Ball $d LP tracklist. On Spotify $WAMI$ it is the album closer (track 18). Venue split on the album page. Bridging cut between the 2018 collage and the 2020 cartoon-rap record.
