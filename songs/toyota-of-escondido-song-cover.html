Toyota of (Escondido) Song Cover is on the Scoobert Doobert channel. YouTube dates the upload October 9, 2020. It runs 1:10.

The description is his: Hilary from 91X commissioned the cover. Same commission note as Mossy Nissan. In-N-Out names this one next.

A later upload, Toyota of Escondido_different drop, is the December 2021 SoundCloud file. Different length, different note.
