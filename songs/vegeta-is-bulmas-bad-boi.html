Content

Track three on the Bandcamp $WAMI$ (LP) (May 9, 2018), about eighteen seconds. Louis Cole sits in on the drum-and-bass moment, same kit credit as Shaggy Connections. The title is the whole gag: Vegeta as Bulma's bad boi, domesticated prince as collage punchline before Dragon Ball $d turns that cosmology into a hip-hopera.

This cut is Bandcamp-only. Spotify's $WAMI$ skips it (and the other Bandcamp-only interludes) and jumps from Phone Plan to Friends. Venue split documented on the album page.
