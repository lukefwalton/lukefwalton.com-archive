Doin' It Right is a FEiN single released November 3, 2017 on Apple Music (℗ 2017 Freeform), distinct from the Tiny Giant–era singles like LOVED and It's Alright. Co-written by Luke Francis Walton and Brandon Michael Woodward.

FEiN wrote this as original music for Freeform, paid through Tiny Giant Recording. The paperwork names Persimmon Grove Music, which is Freeform's publishing catalog, not a TV show. See FEiN commissioned and library work.

Tell Me Lies (Hulu): the same recording appears in the series premiere (S1 E1, 2022) per TuneFind. Same mix, not a new recording.

Not on Little Homes. Other FEiN placements (Lexus, Good Trouble) are on Music → Sync & placements, with sources on Press.
