hostNote
**TOMMASO** (Tommaso Veronesi) is a Chicago songwriter, producer, and drummer. Scoobert collab on **While Away**. Same orbit: he produced **Chan Fuze** singles **This Is Not Cool** and **Methadone Nights** (2023); Walton mixed both. We have mixed together and hung out in the city. The episode title is his session habit: **go-to fills and patterns**. We start where his production ear comes from. He barely had theory; he had drums, and he thought in **energy** (how the song moves forward) long before he had other instruments. We talk **Ringo-y** fills that stay simple on purpose, muscle memory as teaching ("a hundred times is really like nothing"), and a hot take that puts **Meg White** next to Ringo: the drummer who is the song without winning a rudiment contest. After years of weekend gigs he moved back to Chicago and started producing again; the solo project exists as a no-compromise lane. Identity lagged the work: singer, drummer, songwriter, producer. Now he can just say producer and musician. The closer is the Love Music Why question. He does not know who he is without music. He lives it until the job title is almost beside the point.

faq
  • question
    What does TOMMASO mean by go-to patterns?
    answer
    In sessions he reaches for fills and drum beats he already trusts (tasteful, often simple, often Ringo-like) instead of inventing a new trick every bar. The patterns are how a drummer-turned-producer keeps energy moving without overplaying.
  • question
    Why does TOMMASO think drummers make good producers?
    answer
    He came in from the kit with almost no theory. What he had was a say in the energy: how the song moves forward, how the kick and snare sit, how a fill should be the simplest thing that still feels like the best fill. Groove and dynamics were the production ear before other instruments arrived.
  • question
    How is TOMMASO connected to Scoobert Doobert?
    answer
    They are Chicago friends who have mixed and collaborated, including While Away (TOMMASO × Scoobert Doobert), and two 2023 Chan Fuze singles Veronesi produced and Walton mixed (This Is Not Cool, Methadone Nights). The interview is that relationship on tape, not a first meeting. The week before, the solo episode To Be like Water, Take a Nap pointed at this conversation from the drummer's chair.
