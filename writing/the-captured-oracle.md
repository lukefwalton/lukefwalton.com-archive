abstract: "Answer-engine optimization is SEO for large language models. Ask an answer engine a question with a contested answer and it may return a verdict—composed in the engine’s own voice, sources nested in the answer—with no visible author. An optimizer can constitute the frame the engine voices as its own. A public acts on these verdicts with no author reachable: that is the wrong at the answer end. That the engine is only a tool leaves the case standing: where a verdict has been captured, the frame it voices is someone’s, whom the channel conceals, leaving the user the only party in view. An interested frame voiced as impartial synthesis is the wrong’s sharpest form: it survives accuracy and disclosure. Given such a frame, what varies is one thing: the agency of whoever executes it. At the answer end a person acts on a verdict whose frame was hidden; the hidden author must answer. At the action end an AI agent acts in their place: the executor cannot answer, and what remains is the deployer’s choice to make a tool an actor. The remedy is answerable legibility: a frame someone owns and stands behind. The disclosure that would secure it must make legible not the sources cited or the process that produced the text, but who owns the evaluative frame the engine voices."

## Key terms

Each term is given in the paper's own words, quoted from the accepted manuscript.

- **Answer-engine optimization (AEO).** “Answer-engine optimization is SEO for large language models.” (Abstract)
- **Verdict channel.** “the public-facing layer where an engine returns, in its own name, the evaluative judgment a person would otherwise have been answerable for making.” (Section 1)
- **Evaluative frame.** “The frame, here and throughout, is the specification of what counts as signal, relevance, success, and absence in answering the question, including the weighting that settles which answer issues.” (Section 2)
- **Capture.** “Capture is the condition in which the resulting frame is sufficiently constituted by the authored element to carry the interested judgment.” (Section 3)
- **Answer end and action end.** “At the answer end a person acts on a verdict whose frame was hidden; the hidden author must answer. At the action end an AI agent acts in their place: the executor cannot answer, and what remains is the deployer’s choice to make a tool an actor.” (Abstract)
- **Answerable legibility.** “The remedy is answerable legibility: a frame someone owns and stands behind.” (Abstract)

## Technical implementation

The technical implementation is **Answer Engine** — documented in [*Answer Engine: A Small Reference Implementation for Citation-Grounded AI Answers*](/writing/answer-engine/) (technical note v1.1, June 2026). The note states the design contract, evaluation harness, and scope.

It comprises [**answer-engine**](https://github.com/lukefwalton/answer-engine) v1.0.0 ([software DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20676773) · [GitHub](https://github.com/lukefwalton/answer-engine) · [Apache-2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)), the teaching-sized clone-and-run repository, and [Ask the Archive](/ask/about/) on lukefwalton.com, the live deployment behind this site's search.

This paper diagnoses covert authorship on the **verdict channel**; Answer Engine is the contrasting design: the authorial frame stays outside the model, citations must resolve to retrieved evidence, and refusals are tested.
